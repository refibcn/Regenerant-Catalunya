## 1. Executive Summary

- Most projects have clear narratives and relevant deliverables, but indicators are not consistently visible in Karma.
- Evidence is often strong, yet some links are internal or not directly verifiable.
- Standardizing reporting structure and duplicating metrics in descriptions will improve clarity even while the Karma bug persists.

---

## 2. Scope and Sources

- Scope: 11 projects across La Fundicio / Keras Buti and Miceli Social.
- Source: Activities reported in Karma GAP only.
- Feedback index: `03 Libraries/Regenerant-Catalunya/content/es/recursos/feedback-karma/index.md`

---

## 3. Cross-Project Patterns

- Indicators are often defined but not visible (likely due to the Karma bug).
- Activities that group multiple events rarely include a dated list of events.
- Some deliverables are labeled generically, which reduces clarity.
- Continuous activities do not always specify periodicity.

---

## 4. Evidence and Link Quality

- Many projects provide strong visual proof (Instagram, YouTube, Drive).
- Several activities reference "Carpeta interna" or placeholder links.
- Best practice: direct links to a specific asset or public folder.

---

## 5. Metrics Visibility Bug

- Teams report that values were entered, but Karma does not display them.
- Recommendation: duplicate key numbers inside the activity description or deliverable notes until the bug is resolved.

---

## 6. Network-Specific Notes

**La Fundicio / Keras Buti**
- Strong community activation and visible events.
- Main gap: numeric indicators and dated event lists.

**Miceli Social**
- Strong knowledge outputs and governance work.
- Main gap: internal links and missing metrics visibility.

---

## 7. Recommended Internal Actions

1. Keep the standardized feedback format across all projects (context, strengths, improvements, per-activity notes, mini-example, checklist).
2. Share the visibility bug note with all projects to prevent confusion about missing numbers.
3. Encourage a short, dated event list when activities include multiple moments.
4. Prefer direct evidence links over internal placeholders.
