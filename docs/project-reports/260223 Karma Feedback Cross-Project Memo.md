## 1. Executive Summary

- Most projects have clear narratives and relevant deliverables, but numbers are not always surfaced in the first line of each activity.
- Evidence is often strong, yet some links are internal or not directly verifiable.
- Standardizing reporting structure and surfacing key numbers in each activity will improve clarity and comparability.

---

## 2. Scope and Sources

- Scope: 11 projects across La Fundicio / Keras Buti and Miceli Social.
- Source: Activities reported in Karma GAP only.
- Feedback index: `03 Libraries/Regenerant-Catalunya/content/es/recursos/feedback-karma/index.md`

---

## 3. Cross-Project Patterns

- Numbers exist but are not always visible in the activity headline.
- Activities that group multiple events rarely include a dated list of events.
- Some deliverables are labeled generically, which reduces clarity.
- Continuous activities do not always specify periodicity.

---

## 4. Evidence and Link Quality

- Many projects provide strong visual proof (Instagram, YouTube, Drive).
- Several activities reference internal folders without a public link.
- Best practice: direct links to a specific asset or public folder.

---

## 5. Network-Specific Notes

**La Fundicio / Keras Buti**
- Strong community activation and visible events.
- Main gap: numbers and dates are not always placed in the first line.

**Miceli Social**
- Strong knowledge outputs and governance work.
- Main gap: internal links and lack of direct evidence URLs.

---

## 6. Recommended Internal Actions

1. Keep the standardized feedback format across all projects (context, logros, lectura del relato, mejoras, per-activity notes, mini-example, checklist).
2. Encourage a short, dated event list when activities include multiple moments.
3. Prefer direct evidence links over internal placeholders.
4. Put key numbers in the first line of each activity to improve scannability.
