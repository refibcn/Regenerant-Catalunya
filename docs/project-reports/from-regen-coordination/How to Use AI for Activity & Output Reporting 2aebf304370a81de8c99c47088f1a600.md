# **Introduction**

AI tools like **ChatGPT**, **Claude**, and others can be valuable assistants in drafting **Activity & Output reports**. By providing structured guidance, AI can help format and flesh out reports clearly, ensure alignment with reporting best practices, and save time.

This guide will show you how to **leverage AI** effectively, including:

✅ **How to prompt AI to generate structured reports**

✅ **Key guidance to improve accuracy and alignment**

✅ **A ready-to-use AI prompt** to copy and paste

## **How AI Can Help with Reporting**

AI can assist in the **Activity & Output reporting process** by:

- **Structuring your report** based on best practices.
- **Helping refine descriptions** to make them clear, concise, and aligned with reporting requirements.
- **Ensuring outputs are properly categorized** as **Deliverables** (tangible results) or **Metrics** (quantitative indicators).
- **Enhancing clarity & consistency** to avoid vague or unclear submissions.

⚠️ **Remember**: AI **should not** replace your judgment! Carefully **review** and **edit** AI-generated drafts to ensure accuracy and alignment with your project’s real contributions.

# Prompting the AI

## **Step 1 - Write a Clear & Detailed Activity Description**

To ensure that the AI generates a **well-structured and accurate Activity & Output report**, it's important to **provide a detailed and specific activity description**. If you have already written about this activity elsewhere (e.g., in a proposal, blog post, or internal report), **retrieve that information** and use it to enrich your response.

### **Key Guidelines for a Strong Activity Description**

- ✅ **Be specific about what your team/project did**
    - Clearly state the actions taken and the processes followed.
    - If multiple steps were involved, outline them logically.
    - **Example:** Instead of just saying, *"We organized a climate action event,"* provide details:
        - *"Our team planned and executed a climate action workshop that brought together 50 local leaders. We designed the agenda, created educational materials, secured funding, and coordinated logistics to ensure smooth execution."*
- ✅ **Differentiate your contributions from external partners**
    - If you collaborated with other organizations, **explain what you did vs. what they did.**
    - This ensures clarity on the **unique role of your project** in achieving the results.
    - **Example:** *"Our team developed the curriculum and facilitated the sessions, while our partner organization handled outreach and participant registration."*
- ✅ **Explain the purpose & alignment with project goals**
    - How does this activity contribute to your mission?
    - If relevant, mention how it aligns with Regen Coordination's **North Star Outcomes**.
- ✅ **If possible, provide proof or supporting links**
    - If a blog post, social media update, or event recording exists, **include a link** in your response.
    - AI can use this information to **enrich the description further**.

### **Example Input for AI Prompt**

🔹 **Weak Description:**

*"We hosted an event about regenerative finance."*

🔹 **Stronger Description:**

*"We hosted a **ReFi knowledge-sharing workshop** aimed at increasing awareness and engagement in regenerative finance. Our team developed **custom workshop materials**, organized expert speakers, and coordinated outreach to bring in over **50 participants**. The event focused on practical applications of ReFi, and we provided hands-on training on using Web3 tools. We also recorded the session and created a summary report to share key learnings with the broader community."*

This will **significantly improve the accuracy and quality** of your AI-generated report. 🎯

### **Where to Find Activity Information?**

If you've already documented this activity somewhere else, **pull from these sources to enhance accuracy**:

🔹 Previous reports, proposals, or funding applications

🔹 Blog posts or social media announcements

🔹 Event pages, recordings, or presentations

🔹 Internal planning documents or meeting notes

**Copy relevant details from these sources and include them in your AI input.** The more context you provide, the better AI can generate a structured and meaningful report. 🚀

## **Step 2 - Use This Prompt to Generate a Karma GAP Aligned Report**

💡 Copy & paste the following **AI prompt** into ChatGPT, Claude, or another AI tool to generate a structured Activity & Output report.
**‼️** (make sure to replace - `[Insert detailed description of your activity here. ]*`)

```markdown
I need help drafting an Activity & Output report following a structured format.

**Here’s what you need to know:**

- An **Activity** is a key action my project undertook to achieve its goals. The description should clearly detail **what we did**, the specific processes involved, and **how we contributed** to achieving results. **Make it clear what my project directly contributed** versus what external partners or collaborators did.
- **Outputs** are the **direct, tangible results** of the activity. **Outputs may evolve over time** as the activity progresses. Include only **significant outputs** that demonstrate clear results.
- **Deliverables** are **specific, tangible products or results** achieved by the activity. **These should have proof links or supporting evidence.**
- **Metrics** are **quantitative indicators** that measure the direct results of the activity. These show the **scale, reach, or completion level** of the activity.

**Respond in the chat and follow this exact structure for the output, do not use linebreaks or heading:**

## `Activity Name*`
- **Description:** `[Describe what you did, detailing key processes, specific contributions, and distinguishing your work from partners/collaborators.]`
- **Start date:** **`[When did the activity begin?]`**
- **End date:** **`[When did it end? Leave blank if ongoing.]`**
- **Outputs:**
    - **Deliverable:**
        - **Name:** `[What was created?]`
        - **Proof/Link:** `[Where can it be found?]`
        - **Description/Comment:** `[Optional explanation of the deliverable.]`
    - **Metrics:**
        - **Output/Indicator:** `[What are you measuring?]`
        - **Description/Comment:** `[What the metric means or other details/context.]`
        - **Value:** `[Actual measurement.]`
        - **Proof/Link:** `[Evidence supporting this metric.]`

**Guidance for AI:**

- Write in a **concise, professional** tone.
- Ensure **outputs** are **direct results** of the activity.
- Ensure **metrics** are **quantifiable and clearly linked to outputs.**
- Avoid vague descriptions—be **specific** about what was done and achieved.

**Here is my activity:**

- `[Insert detailed description of your activity here. ]*`
```

## **Step 3 - Review & Edit the AI’s Response**

Once AI generates the **draft report**, **carefully review it** and make necessary refinements:

✔ **Ensure accuracy** – AI-generated text may need corrections to better reflect your work.

✔ **Check clarity** – Make sure the **Activity Description** clearly explains your team’s direct contributions.

✔ **Validate outputs & metrics** – Ensure **outputs are actual results** and **metrics are properly quantified**.

✔ **Add missing details** – If the AI omits key proof links or evidence, manually add them.

---

## **Best Practices**

✅ **Use AI for drafting, but refine with human oversight.**

✅ **Keep descriptions focused on your direct contributions.**

✅ **Ensure outputs are tangible and provable.**

✅ **Use clear, quantifiable metrics.**

✅ **Provide proof links whenever possible.**

By using AI tools like **ChatGPT** or **Claude**, you can **streamline the Activity & Output reporting process**, ensuring well-structured, detailed, and aligned reports. However, always **review and refine AI-generated drafts** to ensure they accurately reflect your project’s work.

This approach helps make reporting **faster, more consistent, and more effective**—ultimately improving how projects track and communicate their impact. 🚀
