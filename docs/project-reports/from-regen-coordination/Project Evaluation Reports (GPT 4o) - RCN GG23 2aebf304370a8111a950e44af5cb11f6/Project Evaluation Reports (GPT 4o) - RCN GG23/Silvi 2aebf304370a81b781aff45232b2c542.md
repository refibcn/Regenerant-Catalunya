## 📉 3. **Missing, Incomplete, or Questionable Data**

Several areas of missing or incomplete data limit evaluability:

- **Activity timestamps**: No specific dates or timeframes are attached to many of the activities described (e.g., when were the 150k trees propagated? Over what period were 200+ users onboarded?).
- **Deliverable proof links**: References are made to app launches, partnerships, MRV activities, and Operation WebTree participation — but **no linked outputs** (e.g., app analytics, operational dashboards, nursery tracking sheets, reports).
- **Quantitative metrics**:
    - 200+ users and 150k trees propagated are quoted, but no raw data, documentation, or dashboards are provided.
    - No public reporting on Operation WebTree Phase 1 grant recipients beyond mentioning numbers.
    - No granular reporting of verified reforestation outcomes (e.g., survival rates, hectares restored).
- **Unsubstantiated claims**:
    - Strong claims around engagement with Kenya’s Forestry Service and plans for formal planting rights in Kakamega Forest — **no MoUs, agreements, or letters of support** attached for verification.

**Recommendations**:

- Provide raw metrics dashboards where possible.
- Attach or link deliverable proofs (app screenshots, beta feedback forms, user surveys, event logs).
- Include clearer activity dates.
- Document pilot outcomes more precisely (e.g., # saplings survived after x months).

## 🧭 4. **Alignment with Impact Reporting Best Practices**

Partial alignment:

- **Activities** are reasonably well described (e.g., Operation WebTree, Kenya nurseries, Silvi App launch).
- **Outputs** are inconsistently captured:
    - Some clear outputs (Silvi App, Treekipedia Methodology Configurator) are mentioned but without linked proof.
    - No full breakdown of Output Deliverables and Output Metrics per activity.
- **Attribution vs Collaboration**: Handled appropriately — Silvi generally credits partnerships (e.g., AgroforestDAO, Kokonut Network) and does not over-claim their contributions to other groups' outcomes.

To meet best practices fully:

- Explicitly separate **Activity** (what was done) from **Output Deliverable** (what was produced) and **Output Metric** (how much was achieved).
- Link deliverables where possible to increase verifiability.
- Provide event attendance numbers, user activation data, ecosystem integration stats more systematically.
