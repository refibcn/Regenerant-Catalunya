# 🌱 Evaluation Scores & Rationale

## 🔹 **Criterion 1: Increase Awareness, Engagement, Adoption, and Development of ReFi Web3**

### **Bloom Network Score: 8/10**

### **Rationale**:

Bloom has demonstrated **exceptional engagement and ecosystem development** in ReFi Web3 post-August 2024. Key outputs include:

- Over **300 on-chain impact reports** from local hubs posted to Bloom and automatically synced with Karma GAP.
- Seamless Web3 onboarding of **250+ new wallets** using Para Wallet, across Optimism, Base, and Arbitrum.
- Launch of a **reputation + bounty smart contract system (RAP)** with impact verifiers and payout structures for regenerative actions.
- Integration with Inverter Network and Karma GAP significantly **reduced friction for non-technical users**, while **incentivizing real-world ReFi work**.

They’ve activated and supported **32 organizations** in registering and managing bounties, completed **302 local ReFi bounties**, and enabled **rich impact reporting** across their 30,000-member network.

The only reason this does not receive a 9 or 10 is due to relatively modest scale in developer-specific tooling or protocol-level infrastructure, and the high ratio of qualitative (vs. technical) contribution. Still, this is among the **top quartile of engagement and onboarding efforts** in the round.

# 🔬 Further Evaluation & Feedback

**Project: Bloom Network**

---

## 🧩 1. **Overall Reporting Quality**

Bloom Network’s reporting is among the strongest in the round in terms of breadth, clarity, and alignment with Regen Coordination’s North Star Outcomes. Activities are well-organized and scoped across clearly defined categories (e.g., Local Action Rewards, Web3 integrations, creative outreach), with all major reports supported by **proof links**, measurable metrics, and timestamps.

The reporting distinguishes major streams of work:

- Platform development and Web3 integrations
- Local Bloom node activities and support
- Creative and communications outreach
- Funding/grants efforts
- Governance and cooperative infrastructure

The reports successfully communicate Bloom's contribution to:

- **ReFi Web3 awareness and adoption**: through wallet onboarding, DAO tooling, and social UX
- **Ethereum-aligned financial activity**: via bounty payouts and Karma GAP integrations
- **Local ecological/social/economic impact**: with real-world impact like 7,000 farms started and 400 watershed projects

Overall, the quality of reporting reflects **internal alignment**, strong team coordination, and a clear effort to meet or exceed the expectations of funders and evaluators.

---

## ✂️ 2. **Opportunities for Streamlining and Simplification**

Bloom has done an excellent job of **aggregating data where appropriate**. For example:

- The “Aggregated Bloom Network Impacts” report summarizes Local Bloom activity without unnecessary fragmentation.
- The “Web3 Integrations” report nests multiple sub-activities (Inverter smart contracts, Karma GAP, wallet onboarding) under one clear heading.

We recommend **no further simplification** — the granularity is appropriate for the scale of the project. However, continued **nesting of future reports** (e.g., quarterly summaries of Local Action Rewards rounds) could help keep volume manageable as the number of Bloom hubs grows.

---

## 📉 3. **Missing, Incomplete, or Questionable Data**

There are few instances of vague or incomplete data, but we suggest the following refinements:

- **Community training metrics** (reported as “10”) could benefit from more clarity:
    - What constitutes one “training”? Was it a session, a cohort, or a post?
    - Adding links to specific training content (e.g., videos, slides) would help.
- **Organizations onboarded** and **onchain users** are listed with strong numbers (e.g., 230 wallets, 32 orgs), but could be improved with a **dashboard or visualized trend data** (e.g., month-over-month growth, returning users, user engagement after onboarding).
- Some **impact reports** rely on external links (e.g., [bloomnetwork.earth/communityposts](https://bloomnetwork.earth/communityposts)), which are rich in narrative and visuals but would benefit from **structured tagging or metadata** (e.g., date, location, type of action).

No major overclaims were detected. Bloom is generally **cautious and appropriate in attributing credit**, regularly referencing peer and partner contributions (e.g., Inverter, Para wallet, dOrg, Giveth).

---

## 🧭 4. **Alignment with Impact Reporting Best Practices**

Bloom clearly distinguishes:

- **Activities** (e.g., integrating smart contracts, running a training, distributing bounties)
- **Output Deliverables** (e.g., live integrations, published trainings, verified reports, platform features)
- **Output Metrics** (e.g., 302 bounties completed, 250 wallets created, 30 social posts, $15K+ distributed)

Their structure matches the Regen Coordination guidance extremely well. Reports include:

- Timestamps
- Links to outputs
- Descriptions of what was done
- Metrics tied to outputs
- Clear delineation of who did what

They make appropriate claims about **attribution** and **collaboration**, consistently acknowledging their ecosystem partners.

---

## 🔻 5. **Note areas where the project lost marks or could have gained further points**

- **Ethereum-aligned economic reporting**: More detailed tracking of **Total Value Flowed (TVF)** or transaction-level analytics (e.g., how much capital flowed via Bloom’s smart contracts, across which chains) could strengthen their score under Criterion 2.
- **Analytics dashboard**: Creating a **public analytics dashboard** (tracking wallet growth, report submissions, bounty flow) would help funders and ecosystem allies better visualize ongoing traction.
    - **Suggested next step**: Consider leveraging Karma GAP to implement a public-facing "Activity Summary Dashboard" that aggregates key metrics, highlights major milestones, and shows progress toward annual goals. This would enhance visibility and funder confidence while reinforcing impact transparency.
- **Geospatial mapping** of impacts (e.g., where the 7,000 farms are located, or which hubs are active) could further elevate their impact reporting.
