# 🔬 Further Evaluation & Feedback

## 🧩 1. **Overall Reporting Quality**

GreenPill Ottawa’s reporting is readable, engaging, and clearly demonstrates an active and values-aligned local ReFi initiative. The three submitted activity reports are relatively well-structured and concise, each highlighting a distinct and relevant area of work. The formatting and descriptions are simple but effective in communicating high-level contributions. These activities map well to Regen Coordination’s **North Star Outcomes**, particularly:

- **Awareness & Engagement** through education and event sponsorships,
- **Adoption** via wallet onboarding and integration with Silvi Protocol on Celo,
- **Ecological impact** through reforestation campaigns and community engagement.

The narrative of their contribution is coherent, and their documentation conveys a strong sense of purpose and action — particularly within the Ottawa region. However, the level of detail varies across reports, and the project could improve clarity and evaluability by adding more consistent formatting, timestamps, and structured proof links across entries.

## ✂️ 2. **Opportunities for Streamlining and Simplification**

The current activity set is **appropriately scoped and not over-reported**. Each of the three submitted reports captures a distinct thematic effort (reforestation, university outreach, and institutional onboarding), making them well-suited for separate entries.

If future reports include multiple related workshops (e.g., recurring onboarding sessions, community tree planting events), these could be **grouped under a single "Seasonal Learning Series" or "Operation Web Tree Campaign"** with nested sub-activities to reduce fragmentation while still tracking engagement.

## 📉 3. **Missing, Incomplete, or Questionable Data**

Several opportunities exist for improving **evaluability**:

- **Lack of timestamps**: Reports don’t always specify exact dates for when activities occurred. This limits the ability to assess recency or track pacing against milestones.
- **Unlinked outputs**: There are several mentions of deliverables (e.g., workshops, ticket sponsorships, crypto onboarding resources), but few include direct links to slides, photos, wallet analytics, or community posts to verify engagement.
- **Vague metrics**:
    - “500 saplings purchased” is strong, but **follow-up metrics** like "# planted", "# tracked via Silvi", or "# wallets onboarded" are missing or implied.
    - No metrics were included for the **Regeneration Canada onboarding**, such as number of attendees or progress status of crypto integrations.
    - **Carleton University sponsorship** could include event photos, student feedback, or metrics on continued engagement post-screening.

**Recommendation**: Add brief tables or bullets within each activity to track:

- Number of participants
- Number of wallets created or used
- Number of trees planted vs. committed
- Proof of deliverables (Notion pages, screenshots, social posts, app analytics, etc.)

## 🧭 4. **Alignment with Impact Reporting Best Practices**

GreenPill Ottawa generally respects the reporting distinctions outlined in Regen Coordination’s guidance:

- **Activities** (e.g., hosting onboarding workshops, partnering on Operation Web Tree, sponsoring events) are clearly described.
- **Output deliverables** are mentioned but **underlinked** — more tangible documentation is needed for each (e.g., PDFs, photo albums, app screenshots, wallet stats).
- **Output metrics** are inconsistently applied — some are missing altogether (e.g., number of planters onboarded to Silvi, number of users supported through workshops), and none are structured in a standardized way.

Attribution is generally handled responsibly — co-hosted events and partner-led programs are acknowledged appropriately. That said, it would help to **explicitly separate GreenPill Ottawa’s role** in collaborative efforts (e.g., “we provided onboarding facilitation, partner led the logistics”) to avoid any possible overstatement.
