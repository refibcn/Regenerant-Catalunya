## Additional Analysis on TVF

### ✅ What Greenpill Uganda Is Proposing:

They are building a **crypto-to-fiat off-ramping platform**, enabling users (especially in Uganda and the diaspora) to **convert crypto into Ugandan Shillings (UGX)**. This serves:

- **Remittances**
- **Local payments**
- And crucially, funds public goods (tree planting, education) via a **30% profit reinvestment model**

### 🔁 But What About Web3 Onboarding and TVL?

Here’s the tension:

### ✅ Positive:

- The platform is **Web3-native in its infrastructure** — accepting crypto, using blockchain rails.
- It opens a **regulated, compliant, and locally embedded onramp/offramp** that could build **trust and usability** for Web3 tools.
- If users receive funds *through* the platform (e.g., for regenerative work), and interact with wallets, dApps, or ReFi tooling before off-ramping, that could represent **onboarding + ReFi engagement**.

### ⚠️ Risk:

- If users **immediately off-ramp to UGX**, with no other interaction, this leads to **value extraction from the ecosystem** — a **net negative** in terms of **TVL** and **Web3 stickiness**.
- If most of the value flows *out* rather than *through* ReFi protocols (e.g., governance, QF rounds, stablecoins, local credit pools), it fails to build **Web3-native economic loops**.
- The current documentation lacks any **TVF tracking**, onchain wallet metrics, or designs to **incentivize value staying on-chain**.

---

### 🔍 Strategic Evaluation:

Greenpill Uganda could become **valuable public goods infrastructure** *if*:

- They **build in more "through-Web3" activity** (e.g., earn-to-onboard flows, stablecoin use before exit).
- They **track and report TVF**, not just volumes off-ramped.
- They **support DAO tools**, **local currencies**, or **community treasuries** on-chain.

Otherwise, as currently framed, it risks replicating the **Web3 → Fiat → Gone** pattern — undermining long-term ecosystem sustainability.

# 🔥 Recommendations for Net-Positive TVF and Stronger ReFi Impact

## 1. **Incentivize Pre-Offramp ReFi Interaction**

> Before users cash out to UGX, ensure they interact meaningfully with Web3 rails.
> 

✅ **Ideas**:

- **Impact Wallet Milestones**: Reward users for engaging in activities like participating in public goods voting (e.g., via Gitcoin Passport, Impact Certificates, or similar) before being eligible to off-ramp.
- **Community Pools**: Require interaction with a **local stablecoin**, **community treasury**, or **impact pool** (e.g., pooled funds for schools, trees, etc.) before enabling UGX withdrawal.
- **Micro QF Rounds**: Run **small local quadratic funding rounds** where diaspora remittance senders can allocate part of their remittance to local regenerative projects **onchain** before off-ramping.

---

## 2. **Embed Local Stablecoins or Impact Credits**

> Reduce immediate offramping by creating local Web3-native units of account.
> 

✅ **Ideas**:

- Introduce a **local stablecoin** (pegged to UGX) users can transact with **before** they choose to off-ramp.
- Issue **Impact Credits** — tokens representing contributions to tree planting, education, women's groups — that can be held, traded, or pooled.
- Partner with Celo, Glo Dollar, or other mission-aligned stablecoin projects to anchor a local circular economy.

---

## 3. **Track and Publish Total Value Flow (TVF) Metrics**

> Move beyond just transaction volume (TVL) to capture how much value circulates inside Web3 before fiat exit.
> 

✅ **Ideas**:

- Dashboard showing:
    - % of value held in local stablecoins before offramp
    - number of users participating in onchain voting or local ReFi pools
    - Average holding time before offramp
- Create **TVF per User** metrics to showcase Web3 engagement depth over time.
- Publish monthly or quarterly transparency reports with anonymized flow data.

---

## 4. **Offer Regenerative Yield or Savings Mechanisms**

> Incentivize users to leave some value on-chain.
> 

✅ **Ideas**:

- Partner with DeFi protocols or launch simple staking pools where users **earn extra rewards** (small APYs) for holding assets for longer before offramping.
- Launch "Save to Regenerate" programs: a % of saved crypto funds triggers donations to public goods projects (e.g., 1 month holding = 1 tree planted).

---

## 5. **Create Local Web3 Earning and Spending Opportunities**

> Expand the value users see inside the ecosystem — not just extract-and-exit.
> 

✅ **Ideas**:

- Pay tree planters, women’s groups, or teachers in **crypto or stablecoins** first — and let them decide when/how to off-ramp.
- Support **local onchain marketplaces** where crypto can be spent directly for goods or services (school supplies, farming inputs, etc.).
- Build mini-grant programs paid in crypto to local regenerative projects.
