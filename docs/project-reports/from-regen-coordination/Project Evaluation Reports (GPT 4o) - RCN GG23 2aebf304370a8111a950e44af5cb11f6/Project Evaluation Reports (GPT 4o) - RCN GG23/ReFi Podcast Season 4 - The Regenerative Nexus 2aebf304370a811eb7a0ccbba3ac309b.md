    - Suggested: Provide visual metrics screenshots or back-end analytics where available.
- **Potential Overlap in Audience Metrics**:
    - 86,000 episode views across Spotify, Apple, and YouTube was cited, but it’s unclear if this represents **unique listeners** vs. total plays across platforms.
    - Suggested: Clarify how duplicate listening across platforms is handled, if possible.
- **Missing Timeline Context**:
    - While dates are given for most activities, it would help to **anchor the reporting with a short overall timeline** summarizing when Season 4 ran (e.g., May 2024 to April 2025) so the evaluator immediately understands the time scope at a glance.

**Conclusion**: Good data, but can tighten attribution metrics and proof robustness slightly.
