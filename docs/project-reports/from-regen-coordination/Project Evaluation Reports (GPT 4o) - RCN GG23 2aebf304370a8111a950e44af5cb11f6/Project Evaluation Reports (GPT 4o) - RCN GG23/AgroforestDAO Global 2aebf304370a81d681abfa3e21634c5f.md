## 🧩 1. **Overall Reporting Quality**

AgroforestDAO Global has submitted one of the most **comprehensive, coherent, and evaluable** sets of activity and output reports in this cohort. The reporting structure is impressively detailed and spans the full breadth of project activity — from educational journeys and onchain coordination tools to IRL agroecological actions and partnership events.

### Strengths:

- **Clarity & Chronology**: Reports are timestamped, presented in clear chronological order, and anchored to real-world activities (e.g., JA2 Learning Journey sessions from March to April 2025).
- **Output-linked Proof**: Each session or campaign includes **direct links** to documents, POAPs, social media, and tooling dashboards — a key practice that supports transparency and evaluability.
- **Relevance to North Stars**: Reports show consistent and explicit alignment with Regen Coordination’s **North Star Outcomes**:
    - **ReFi Web3** adoption (wallet onboarding, POAPs, use of Sarafu, Giveth/Gitcoin, Charmverse)
    - **Ethereum ecosystem integration** (Celo token usage, Sarafu Pools, NFTs on Celo)
    - **Local impact** (Redemption Hill planting, volunteer hosting, seed swaps, toolkits for agroforestry stewards)

### Key Takeaway:

This is a standout example of grassroots, multilingual, on-the-ground regenerative reporting that bridges the digital and ecological — enabling a full-picture understanding of both Web3 adoption and local outcomes.

---

## ✂️ 2. **Opportunities for Streamlining and Simplification**

AgroforestDAO’s reporting is **largely well-structured**. However, there is some **redundancy and over-fragmentation** around the Learning Journey reports:

### Suggestions:

- **Nest JA2 Sessions**: Reports for each session (1–6) of the second AgroforestDAO Journey could be grouped under one **“JA2 Learning Program”** parent report, with sub-sections for:
    - Session topics
    - POAP links
    - Attendance data
    - Recordings and documents
    - Lessons learned
- **Merge Related Sarafu Pools**: The two reports on Sarafu activity in Jan and Feb 2025 might be better as a single **“Sarafu Commitment Pools”** report that includes onboarding, hosting days, token flows, and emerging learnings from voucher exchange mechanisms.

These changes would reduce repetition and give evaluators a clearer sense of the full arc and cumulative impact of each core initiative.

---

## 📉 3. **Missing, Incomplete, or Questionable Data**

While generally excellent, a few areas limit evaluability:

- **Attendance Variability**: POAPs are used as proxies for session participation, but attendance ranges widely (e.g., 14 minted in Session 1, only 9 in Session 5). It’s unclear if these drops reflect true decline or reporting gaps. A **session-level attendance dashboard** or table would help clarify.
- **Economic Flow Data**: The Sarafu Pool provides hosting day metrics, but doesn’t clarify:
    - How many hosts participated?
    - How much was exchanged in $USDGLO?
    - Whether liquidity matched demand?
    A basic economic flow chart or dashboard would strengthen credibility here.
- **Outcome Evidence for Volunteers**: The project mentions empowerment and network-building, but there’s no data on long-term engagement or satisfaction from volunteers or stewards. A simple **follow-up survey or qualitative feedback summary** would add depth.

---

## 🧭 4. **Alignment with Impact Reporting Best Practices**

AgroforestDAO Global consistently distinguishes between:

- **Activities**: Learning Journey sessions, social campaigns, planting events, protocol integrations
- **Output Deliverables**: POAPs, Charmverse dashboards, Instagram posts, onboarding docs
- **Output Metrics**: Attendees (per session), POAPs minted, number of hosted days (88), voucher transactions (6), applicants (75+)

They excel at **providing proof for outputs** — nearly every claim includes linked evidence.

### Attribution vs. Collaboration:

- The project does **not overclaim credit** for partner work.
- Their reports **clearly identify collaborators** (e.g., NetX State, GreenPill Brazil, AtlantisDAO, Workaway).
- Contributions to events like Ipê City or cross-org QF rounds are framed accurately.

This kind of integrity is essential to ecosystem trust and should be commended.

---

## 📉 5. **Where the Project Lost Marks or Could Improve Further**

### Missed Opportunities:

- **TVF (Total Value Flowed)** data: No tracking of economic flows or onchain user interactions beyond basic POAP and Sarafu Pool data. Even simple CSV summaries of wallet activity, voucher volume, or redeemed services would help demonstrate Web3 economic engagement.
- **Long-term impact indicators**: The project mentions empowerment, coordination, and land stewardship, but could bolster claims with:
    - Retention data from past Learning Journeys
    - Alumni-led projects or re-applications
    - Repeat stewardship by same volunteers
- **Open dashboards**: Charmverse and Sarafu are powerful, but not yet well-linked to live analytics. A **public-facing dashboard** with top-line metrics (wallets onboarded, vouchers exchanged, sessions hosted, hectares stewarded) would enhance transparency and funder confidence.
