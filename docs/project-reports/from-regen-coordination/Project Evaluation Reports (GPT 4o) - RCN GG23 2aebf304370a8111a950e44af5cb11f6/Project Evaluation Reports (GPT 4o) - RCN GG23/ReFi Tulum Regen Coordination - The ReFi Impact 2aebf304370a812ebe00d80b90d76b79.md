# 🔬 Further Evaluation & Feedback

## 🧩 1. **Overall Reporting Quality**

ReFi Tulum’s reporting demonstrates **strong storytelling and activity documentation**, particularly in terms of local ecological actions and cultural engagements. Their reports are **clear**, **structured**, and **chronologically scoped** around distinct activities, making them reasonably easy to evaluate.

Each major report (e.g., Sian Ka'an cleanups, Arte Eterno exhibit) includes:

- A narrative description of the activity,
- A set of output metrics (e.g., volunteers engaged, waste removed),
- Visual proof links (Instagram posts).

**Alignment with North Star Outcomes** is present — especially contributing to **Local Ecological, Social, and Economic Impact** and **ReFi Web3 Awareness** (through the regenerative art exhibition and NFT initiatives). However, explicit references to **Web3 adoption**, **wallet onboarding**, or **blockchain use metrics** are thin, limiting evidence for deeper alignment with **ReFi Web3 development goals**.

Overall, their reporting is **coherent** and **sufficiently detailed**, but could be even stronger with tighter connections between activities and blockchain/Web3-specific metrics.

---

## ✂️ 2. **Opportunities for Streamlining and Simplification**

- **No major fragmentation issues were observed.**
    
    The reports are appropriately grouped by major activities (e.g., each cleanup, each exhibition or activation), and not overly broken down into micro-activities.
    
- **No need for forced nesting.**
    
    Each major event (e.g., beach cleanups, museum exhibits) represents a clear standalone deliverable with distinct dates, locations, and partnerships.
    

**✅ Conclusion**: Reporting is already well-structured. No streamlining needed.

---

## 📉 3. **Missing, Incomplete, or Questionable Data**

- **Some deliverables could benefit from more robust proof links.**
    - Several reports reference Instagram posts. While acceptable for initial proof, **archiving more formal deliverables** (like PDF impact summaries or field data spreadsheets) would strengthen credibility.
- **Limited onchain/Web3 adoption metrics.**
    - For activities promoting NFTs, regenerative finance concepts, or token systems (e.g., TulumCoin DAO partnerships), **no wallet onboarding numbers, no transaction counts**, or **no platform usage analytics** are shared.
- **Attribution vs. collaboration is mostly well handled**, but some actions (e.g., cleanups with 5+ partners) could clarify more precisely **ReFi Tulum’s role** versus partners like Tulum Circula or Feed Tulum.

**Recommendations for improvement:**

- Where Instagram is the primary proof, consider also **publishing brief written post-event summaries** on a public Notion page, GitHub, or project website.
- **Include basic Web3 usage stats** (even if estimated): e.g., # of NFTs minted, wallets onboarded, transactions triggered, etc.
- Explicitly specify roles in partnership initiatives (e.g., “ReFi Tulum led logistics; partner X handled waste hauling”).

---

## 🧭 4. **Alignment with Impact Reporting Best Practices**

- **Activities** (e.g., cleanups, art exhibitions) are clearly described.
- **Output Deliverables** (e.g., waste removed, exhibitions completed) are well documented.
- **Output Metrics** (e.g., kg of waste collected, participants engaged) are provided and generally clear.
- **Attribution** is handled **reasonably well**, though more formal clarity between lead and support roles would improve transparency.

They **did not overclaim impact** and gave appropriate credit to partner organizations where named.

**✅ Strong alignment overall**, with only minor opportunities for improved rigor in proof links and partner role descriptions.

---

## 🔻 5. Areas Where the Project Lost Marks or Could Have Gained Further Points

- **Web3 adoption and ecosystem engagement metrics are weak or absent.**
    - This hurt scoring in both **Criterion 1** (ReFi Web3 engagement) and **Criterion 2** (Ethereum ecosystem contribution).
    - Suggestion: future events or campaigns should **track wallet creation**, **NFT interaction**, or **onchain financial flows** wherever Web3 tools are part of the project narrative.
- **Limited forward planning documentation.**
    - Only one milestone (Earth Day activation) was set for 2025; no quarterly planning, KPIs, or strategic maps were shared.
    - Suggestion: establish a **public-facing 2025 roadmap** with 3–4 milestones, target metrics, and alignment to Regen Coordination goals.
