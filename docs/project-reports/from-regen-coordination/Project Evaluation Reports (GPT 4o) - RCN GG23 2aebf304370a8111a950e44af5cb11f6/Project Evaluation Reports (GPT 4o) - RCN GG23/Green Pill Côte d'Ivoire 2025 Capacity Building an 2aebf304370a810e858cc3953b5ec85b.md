**Summary**: Overall, reporting is decent for an early-stage group — readable, activity-aligned, but could be stronger with clearer, verifiable deliverables.

## 📉 3. **Missing, Incomplete, or Questionable Data**

Several areas of concern:

- **Weak Proof Links**: Many outputs are documented only via Instagram pages (sometimes from ‘habitants.xyz’ rather than GreenPill CIV's direct account), which are *not clearly explained or attributable*. The habitant.xyz account should be clarified or avoided if unrelated.
- **Lack of Hard Metrics for Some Outputs**:
    - No wallet counts, dApp usage, or on-chain actions reported despite ReFi/Web3 framing.
    - Participation numbers are reported for some events but not consistently across all activities.
- **Activity-Output Attribution is Blurry**:
    - Some partnership activities seem to rely on the broader success of events like "Quartier France" without fully clarifying GreenPill CIV’s *direct* contributions.
- **Limited Deliverable Evidence**: No downloadable materials (e.g., agendas, reports, decks) are shared for workshops or conferences.
- **Missing Dates**: Activities reference months but occasionally lack precise event dates.

**Recommendations**:

- Add downloadable proof (e.g., event summaries, presentations).
- Differentiate outputs they led vs. those where they were collaborators.
- Be cautious when using third-party social pages for proof — focus on official GreenPill CIV accounts or clear media.
