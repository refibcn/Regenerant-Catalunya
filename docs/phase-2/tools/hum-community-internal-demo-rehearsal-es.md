# Hum Community Internal Demo - ReFi BCN (ES)
## Internal rehearsal before network demo

**Goal:** Align facilitation, validate the demo flow, and reduce friction before the live session.  
**Duration:** 30-40 min  
**Participants:** Facilitator + co-facilitator + note taker

---

## Pre-work (day before)

- Confirm access to partner portal.
- Ensure demo community is ready.
- Prepare 2-3 fake proposals with budgets.
- Assign roles and decide who says what.

---

## Rehearsal Flow

### 1) 5 min - Quick alignment
- Review the script and run-of-show.
- Confirm who handles onboarding support.

### 2) 10 min - Walkthrough (dry run)
- Facilitator runs the full intro and framing.
- Co-facilitator joins and practices the join flow.

### 3) 10 min - Hands-on test
- Everyone votes on the fake proposals.
- Check that the flow is simple and quick.

### 4) 5-10 min - Fixes
- List any friction or unclear steps.
- Decide edits to the script or join steps.

---

## Internal Checklist

- Demo community access works.
- Join steps are clear and short.
- Fake proposals are ready and understandable.
- We can explain: Hum decides, Safe/bank executes.
- Evidence capture plan is ready (screenshots + notes).
