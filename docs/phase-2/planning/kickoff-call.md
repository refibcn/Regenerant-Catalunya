## Decision Framework

### Tool Selection Criteria

**Considerations:**
1. **Network Culture:** Tech-forward vs cooperative-traditional
2. **Technical Capacity:** Self-manageable vs needs intensive support
3. **Decision-Making Style:** Fast-paced vs deliberative
4. **Implementation Effort:** Low/Medium/High
5. **Support Needs:** Light vs Intensive
6. **Timeline:** 2-3 weeks vs 3-4 weeks

### Implementation Effort Estimates

| Tool | Effort Level | Support Needs | Timeline | Best Fit |
|------|--------------|---------------|----------|----------|
| **Safe Multisig** | Medium | Light | 2-3 weeks | Miceli (tech-forward) |
| **Hum Community** | High | Intensive | 3-4 weeks | La Fundició (cooperative) |
| **IDK Mini-Program** | Medium | Moderate | 3 sessions | Both networks |

### Pivot Options

**If Networks Not Interested in Tools:**
- Focus on IDK mini-program (valuable standalone)
- Governance documentation and mapping
- Reduce scope to what's achievable
- Document learnings for future

**If Implementation More Complex Than Anticipated:**
- Reduce scope to minimum viable implementation
- Extend timeline for quality outcomes
- Focus on one tool rather than multiple
- Document learnings and pivot points
