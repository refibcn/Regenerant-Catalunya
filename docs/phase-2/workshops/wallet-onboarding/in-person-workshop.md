## Workshop Flow

### 1. Welcome & Context (15 minutes)

**Facilitator Notes:**
- Create welcoming, non-intimidating atmosphere
- Emphasize practical benefits for cooperative work
- Address concerns upfront

**Opening (5 min):**

"Bienvenidos y bienvenidas. Thank you for joining us today. We're here to help you open your Valora wallets - this is a practical workshop focused on getting you set up and comfortable with using cryptographic wallets for your work."

**Why Cryptographic Wallets? (5 min):**

"Before we dive into the technical steps, let's talk about why this matters for your work:

- **Autonomy:** You control your funds directly, no intermediaries
- **Security:** Cryptographic security protects your assets
- **Transparency:** All transactions are visible on the blockchain
- **Accessibility:** Works anywhere with internet connection
- **Future-proof:** Web3 tools becoming essential for funding

For cooperative and activist work, wallets give you direct access to funding mechanisms that don't require traditional banking relationships."

**What We'll Do Today (5 min):**

"Here's what we'll accomplish together:

1. Install Valora app on your phones
2. Create your wallets securely
3. Understand seed phrases and security
4. Practice basic operations
5. Get everyone set up for Phase 1 disbursement
6. Take photos for project documentation

We'll go step-by-step, and we're here to help with any questions. There's no rush - we'll make sure everyone is comfortable before moving on."

**Questions Before We Start:**
- "Does everyone have a smartphone with app store access?"
- "Any concerns or questions before we begin?"
- "Does everyone have their phone charged?"

### 3. Wallet Creation & Security (30 minutes)

**Facilitator Notes:**
- This is the critical security section
- Emphasize seed phrase importance multiple times
- Individual support essential
- Document wallet addresses securely

**Creating Your Wallet:**

**Step 1: Open Valora App**

"Open the Valora app you just installed. You'll see a welcome screen."

**Step 2: Create New Wallet**

"Tap 'Create New Wallet' or 'Get Started'"

**Step 3: Accept Terms**

"Read and accept the terms of service. These are standard terms for using the app."

**Step 4: Set Up Recovery (CRITICAL)**

"Valora will ask you to set up recovery. This is how you can recover your wallet if you lose access:

- **Phone Number:** Enter your phone number
- **Email:** Enter your email address
- **Trusted Contacts:** You can add trusted contacts for social recovery

**IMPORTANT:** These recovery methods help, but your seed phrase is the most important backup."

**Step 5: Seed Phrase Generation**

"Valora will generate your seed phrase - this is a list of 12 words that act as your master password.

**CRITICAL SECURITY RULES:**

1. **NEVER share your seed phrase with anyone** - not even us
2. **Write it down on paper** - don't store it digitally
3. **Store it securely** - somewhere safe and private
4. **Don't take photos of it** - photos can be hacked
5. **If you lose it, you lose access** - there's no way to recover without it

Let's write down your seed phrase together. I'll guide you through this step-by-step."

**Individual Seed Phrase Setup:**

[Facilitator and support team help each participant individually]

1. "Valora will show you 12 words - write them down in order"
2. "Double-check spelling - each word matters"
3. "Store the paper securely - this is your backup"
4. "Confirm you've written it down before proceeding"

**Step 6: Verify Seed Phrase**

"Valora will ask you to verify your seed phrase by selecting words in order. This confirms you've written it down correctly."

[Help participants verify their seed phrase]

**Step 7: Set PIN**

"Set a PIN for quick access to your wallet. Choose something you'll remember but others won't guess."

**Step 8: Wallet Created!**

"Congratulations! Your wallet is created. You should see your wallet address - this is like your account number. You can share this address publicly - it's safe to share."

**Collecting Wallet Information:**

"Now we need to collect your wallet addresses for Phase 1 disbursement. We'll do this securely:

1. Write your wallet address on the collection form
2. We'll verify it's correct
3. We'll store it securely for disbursement

**Remember:** Your wallet address is safe to share. Your seed phrase is NOT."

[Collect wallet addresses securely, verify each one]

## Troubleshooting Guide

### Common Issues During Workshop

**Issue: Participant can't install Valora**
- Check phone compatibility
- Verify app store access
- Try alternative device
- Provide manual installation guide

**Issue: Participant lost seed phrase**
- Help them recover using recovery methods
- If recovery fails, need to create new wallet
- Emphasize importance of seed phrase backup

**Issue: Participant can't verify seed phrase**
- Go through words one by one
- Check spelling carefully
- Help them write it down again
- Verify before proceeding

**Issue: Wallet address not showing**
- Check app is fully loaded
- Try closing and reopening app
- Verify wallet creation completed
- Contact technical support if needed

**Issue: Participant concerned about security**
- Reassure about cryptographic security
- Explain seed phrase importance
- Address specific concerns
- Provide additional resources

## Security Best Practices

### Critical Rules

1. **NEVER share seed phrases** - Not via email, chat, or any digital channel
2. **In-person collection preferred** - Most secure method
3. **Wallet addresses are safe to share** - Public information, needed for disbursement
4. **Seed phrases stay private** - Participants write down, team never sees

### What to Collect

**✅ Safe to Collect:**
- Wallet addresses (public addresses)
- Contact information (for support)
- Consent for photos

**❌ Never Collect:**
- Seed phrases (recovery phrases)
- PIN codes
- Private keys
