## Workshop Flow

### 1. Welcome & Context (10 minutes)

**Facilitator Notes:**
- Create welcoming online atmosphere
- Check technical setup
- Emphasize practical benefits

**Opening (3 min):**

"Welcome everyone. Thank you for joining this remote workshop. We're here to help you open your Valora wallets for Phase 1 disbursement.

**Technical Check:**
- Can everyone see my screen?
- Can everyone hear me clearly?
- Does everyone have their smartphone ready?"

**Why Cryptographic Wallets? (4 min):**

"Before we dive in, let's talk about why this matters:

- **Direct Access:** Receive funds without traditional banking
- **Autonomy:** You control your funds directly
- **Security:** Cryptographic security protects your assets
- **Future-Proof:** Web3 tools becoming essential for funding

For your work, wallets give you direct access to funding mechanisms."

**What We'll Do Today (3 min):**

"Here's our plan:

1. Install Valora app on your phones
2. Create your wallets securely
3. Understand seed phrases and security
4. Practice basic operations
5. Collect wallet addresses for Phase 1 disbursement

We'll go step-by-step. Feel free to ask questions in chat or unmute."

### 3. Wallet Creation & Security (20 minutes)

**Facilitator Notes:**
- Critical security section
- Emphasize seed phrase importance
- Individual support via chat/breakout rooms

**Creating Your Wallet:**

**Screen Share: Wallet Creation Process**

"I'll walk you through creating your wallet. Follow along on your phones."

**Step-by-Step:**
1. "Open Valora app"
2. "Tap 'Create New Wallet'"
3. "Accept terms"
4. "Set up recovery (phone number, email)"
5. "Seed phrase generation - CRITICAL STEP"

**Seed Phrase Security (CRITICAL):**

"Your seed phrase is 12 words - this is your master password.

**CRITICAL RULES:**
- NEVER share your seed phrase
- Write it down on paper
- Don't store it digitally
- Don't take photos of it
- If you lose it, you lose access

Let's write it down together. I'll guide you step-by-step."

**Individual Support:**

"If you need help with seed phrase:
- Type 'help' in chat
- We can use breakout rooms for individual support
- Don't rush - this is the most important step"

**Verification:**

"Once you've written down your seed phrase:
- Verify it by selecting words in order
- Double-check spelling
- Confirm before proceeding"

**Collecting Wallet Addresses:**

"Now we need your wallet addresses for Phase 1 disbursement.

**How to find your wallet address:**
1. Open Valora app
2. Tap on your balance or profile
3. Your wallet address is shown there
4. Copy it or write it down

**Share your wallet address:**
- Type it in chat (it's safe to share)
- Or email it to hola@ReFiBCN.cat
- We'll verify it's correct"

[Collect wallet addresses via chat or email]

## Security Best Practices

### Critical Rules

1. **NEVER share seed phrases** - Not via email, chat, or any digital channel
2. **Wallet addresses are safe to share** - Public information, needed for disbursement
3. **Seed phrases stay private** - Participants write down, team never sees

### What to Collect

**✅ Safe to Collect:**
- Wallet addresses (public addresses)
- Contact information (for support)

**❌ Never Collect:**
- Seed phrases (recovery phrases)
- PIN codes
- Private keys
