### NETWORK-SPECIFIC SESSION A: Miceli Social Network

**Network Context (facilitator reminder):**
- **Profile:** Tech-forward, interested in deep Web3 integration
- **Leadership:** Oriol leading, interested in KarmaGap potential
- **Approach:** Open to technical experimentation, hackathon format proposed
- **Projects:** Rural/environmental focus (Regeneració.XYZ, Resilience Earth, De Bat a Bat, Chapter#2, Anigami, Mixité)

#### Activity 1: Current State Mapping (15 min)

**FACILITATOR SAYS:**
> "Let's start by mapping how things currently work. I'm going to ask you some questions, and I want you to think out loud together."

**GUIDED QUESTIONS:**

1. **Decision-Making:**
   - "How are decisions typically made in Miceli Social network?"
   - "Who's involved? Is it consensus? Voting? Informal?"
   - "What types of decisions require network-level coordination?"

2. **Resource Allocation:**
   - "How do resources currently get allocated among network members/projects?"
   - "What's the process? Who decides?"
   - "Is it transparent? Does everyone know how decisions are made?"

3. **Communication & Coordination:**
   - "How does information flow in the network?"
   - "What tools do you use? (WhatsApp, email, meetings?)"
   - "How do you ensure everyone's voice is heard?"

**DO:**
- Capture responses on whiteboard/digital board
- Use sticky notes for individual input first, then discuss
- Draw simple diagrams showing current flows
- Ask clarifying questions
- Notice patterns and themes

#### Activity 2: Challenges & Friction Points (10 min)

**FACILITATOR SAYS:**
> "Now that we've mapped the current state, let's talk about what's not working well. What are the pain points or challenges?"

**PROMPT WITH:**
- "What slows down decision-making?"
- "Where does confusion or misalignment happen?"
- "What opportunities are you missing because of current limitations?"
- "Where do you wish you had better tools or processes?"

**DO:**
- Write down each challenge mentioned
- Group similar challenges together
- Prioritize: "Which of these feels most urgent or impactful?"

**EXAMPLES TO LISTEN FOR:**
- Slow decision-making processes
- Unclear who has authority to decide
- Difficulty tracking what was decided
- Lack of transparency in fund allocation
- Hard to coordinate across projects
- Missing opportunities for collaboration

#### Activity 3: Desired Future State (15 min)

**FACILITATOR SAYS:**
> "Let's dream a bit. If we could wave a magic wand, what would 'better' look like for network governance in Miceli Social?"

**PROMPT WITH:**
- "How would decisions get made in an ideal world?"
- "What would transparency look like?"
- "How would you want to allocate network resources?"
- "What would make collaboration easier?"

**DO:**
- Encourage concrete, specific descriptions
- Draw or diagram the desired future state
- Connect back to challenges: "Would this address the [X] challenge?"
- Identify what Web3 tools might enable

**FOR MICELI SOCIAL SPECIFICALLY:**
> "Oriol, you mentioned interest in exploring KarmaGap integration and technical experimentation. How might that fit into this future vision?"

**FACILITATOR NOTE:**
Miceli network will likely be comfortable with technical language and blockchain concepts. You can reference tools more directly and explore advanced features. They may propose innovative governance mechanisms - encourage this while keeping it grounded in practical needs.

### Tool 1: Safe (Multisigs) - 8 min

### SLIDE 8: Safe Introduction

**[VISUAL: Screenshots of Safe interface, simple transaction flow diagram]**

**SAY:**
> "Safe is a multi-signature wallet - think of it like a shared bank account that requires multiple people to approve before any money can be spent.
>
> Instead of one person controlling funds, you designate key stakeholders - maybe 5 people - and set a threshold, like '3 of 5 must approve.' Any transaction requires multiple signatures before it executes."

**KEY FEATURES:**
- **Multi-signature security:** No single person can move funds alone
- **Transparent on-chain:** All transactions publicly visible on blockchain
- **Flexible thresholds:** Customize how many approvals needed
- **Treasury management:** Track all funds coming in and going out

**SHOW:**
- Quick demo or screenshots of Safe interface
- How a proposal works: propose → others review → sign → execute
- Transaction history view

**ADDRESS NETWORKS DIFFERENTLY:**

**For Miceli Social:**
> "Safe gives you maximum control and transparency. You can see exactly who proposed what, who approved it, when it executed. All recorded permanently on-chain. You could even integrate it with other tools through Safe's SDK."

**For La Fundición:**
> "Think of Safe like a cooperative bank account that requires several board members to sign before spending money - except it's all digital and transparent. Everyone can see what was proposed and who approved it."

**POTENTIAL USE CASES:**
- Network-level fund management
- Project grants/allocations requiring approval
- Paying shared expenses (events, materials, etc.)
- Transparent financial reporting

### SLIDE 9: Safe - When to Use

**SHOW:**
✅ **Good for:**
- Networks that want maximum transparency and security
- Managing significant funds collectively
- Networks comfortable with Web3 wallets
- When multiple decision-makers need approval rights

⚠️ **Consider limitations:**
- Requires all signers to have Web3 wallets
- Blockchain transactions (though costs low on Celo)
- Slightly more technical than some alternatives

### Tool 2: Home Community - 8 min

### SLIDE 10: Home Community Introduction

**[VISUAL: Screenshots of Home Community mobile app interface]**

**SAY:**

**POTENTIAL USE CASES:**

### SLIDE 11: Home Community - When to Use

**[VISUAL: IDK framework diagram, photos of cards if available]**

## Facilitator Tips & Reminders

### General Facilitation

✅ **Do:**
- Hold space for different comfort levels with technology
- Validate all perspectives and concerns
- Use accessible language (avoid jargon)
- Connect back to sense-making throughout
- Be genuinely curious about network needs
- Allow silence for thinking time
- Notice who's not speaking and invite them in

❌ **Don't:**
- Push specific tool preferences
- Dismiss concerns about technology
- Assume technical knowledge
- Rush through sense-making
- Compare networks as better/worse
- Use blockchain jargon unnecessarily

### Network-Specific Considerations

**For Miceli Social:**
- Can use more technical language
- Embrace experimentation mindset
- Connect to broader Web3 ecosystem
- Explore advanced features
- Reference other projects using similar tools

**For La Fundición:**
- Use cooperative/community language
- Emphasize practical benefits over technical features
- Provide lots of reassurance and support
- Reference physical/in-person processes
- Respect skepticism and honor their wisdom

### Managing Group Dynamics

**If one person dominates:**
- "Thank you [name]. Let's hear from others who haven't spoken yet."
- Use structured rounds where everyone speaks

**If discussion stalls:**
- Break into pairs for 2-minute discussions first
- Use specific prompts or examples
- Shift to written brainstorming (sticky notes)

**If tension or disagreement emerges:**
- Acknowledge: "I hear different perspectives emerging."
- Explore: "What's underneath this disagreement?"
- Find common ground: "What do you agree on?"
- Park if needed: "Let's note this and return to it later."

**If energy drops:**
- Take an impromptu 5-minute break
- Change activity format (standing, moving, pairs)
- Shift from discussion to visual/creative
- Acknowledge: "I'm noticing energy shift. Let's [adjust]."

### Time Management

**Running behind:**
- Prioritize sense-making over tool details
- Shorten tool introductions (can share documentation later)
- Extend workshop by 15-20 minutes if possible
- Schedule follow-up session for deeper planning

**Running ahead:**
- Allow more time for questions and discussion
- Go deeper into examples and use cases
- Let networks practice tool workflows
- Extended closing reflection

## Backup Plans

### If Technology Fails

**Hybrid setup issues:**
- Have phone numbers to call in participants
- Use backup video platform (Zoom + Google Meet ready)
- Can proceed in-person only if hybrid impossible
- Record and share with remote participants later

**Projector/screen issues:**
- Have printed slide deck as backup
- Can facilitate without slides (content knowledge)
- Use whiteboard/flip charts for key points

### If Participants Are Missing

**Low attendance:**
- Proceed if at least 3 people per network
- If only one network represented, focus on that network
- Record session for absent participants
- Schedule make-up session if critical mass missing

**Key decision-makers absent:**
- Proceed with planning but note decisions as "provisional"
- Explicit check-in with absent members before finalizing
- Might need follow-up session to confirm

### If Networks Resist Tool Adoption

**Strong resistance in one or both networks:**
- Acknowledge: "It's totally okay to not adopt these tools."
- Explore: "What would need to be different?"
- Alternative: "What if we started with just IDK mapping?"
- Fallback: Phase 2 funds can be allocated without Web3 tools
  - Network makes decisions offline
  - ReFi Barcelona executes based on network decisions
  - Document governance process instead

**Remember:** The goal is supporting networks, not forcing technology adoption.
