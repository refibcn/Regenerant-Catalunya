## Sense-Making Questions

### Current State (15 min)
1. How are decisions made?
2. Who's involved?
3. How do resources get allocated?
4. How does information flow?

### Challenges (10 min)
1. What slows decision-making?
2. Where is confusion/misalignment?
3. What opportunities are missed?
4. Where do you need better tools?

### Desired Future (15 min)
1. How would ideal decision-making work?
2. What would transparency look like?
3. How to allocate resources?
4. What makes collaboration easier?

## Planning Session Guide

### Decisions to Make (30 min)

**1. Tool Selection (10 min)**
- Which tool(s) for Phase 2?
- Why these tools?
- Any to explore further?

**2. Governance Structure (10 min)**
- Who's involved in decisions?
- Safe: Signers + threshold?
- Home: Who can propose/vote?
- Decision scope?

**3. Support Needs (10 min)**
- What training needed?
- Testing/pilot approach?
- Timeline preferences?

---

## Facilitator Reminders

### Do's
✅ Center network needs (not tools)  
✅ Validate all perspectives  
✅ Use accessible language  
✅ Connect to sense-making  
✅ Hold space for skepticism  
✅ Affirm both networks equally

### Don'ts
❌ Push specific tools  
❌ Dismiss technology concerns  
❌ Assume technical knowledge  
❌ Rush sense-making  
❌ Compare networks  
❌ Use unnecessary jargon
