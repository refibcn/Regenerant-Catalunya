## Detailed Comparison

### Safe (Multisig Wallet)

**What It Is:**
A multi-signature wallet that requires multiple people to approve transactions before funds can be moved. Think of it like a shared bank account where several people must sign before spending money.

**Core Features:**
- ✅ Multi-signature security (e.g., 3 of 5 must approve)
- ✅ Transparent on-chain transaction history
- ✅ Flexible approval thresholds
- ✅ Works on Celo network (low fees)
- ✅ Widely adopted, battle-tested
- ✅ Can customize permissions per signer
- ✅ Transaction proposals with comments
- ✅ Integration with other Web3 tools

**How It Works:**
1. Create Safe with designated signers
2. Anyone with address can propose transaction
3. Required number of signers review and approve
4. Once threshold met, transaction executes
5. All activity recorded on blockchain

**Use Cases:**
- Network treasury management
- Project grant approvals
- Paying shared expenses
- Transparent financial governance
- Managing significant sums securely

**Network Fit:**

**Miceli Social:** ★★★ **Excellent fit**
- Technical comfort level matches
- Maximum transparency and control
- Can explore advanced features
- Integrates with other tools

**La Fundición:** ★★ **Possible, but may be complex**
- Requires Web3 wallet knowledge
- More technical than Home Community
- Better as backup or complement to Home Community

**Requirements:**
- Each signer needs Web3 wallet (Valora, MetaMask, etc.)
- Basic understanding of blockchain transactions
- Internet-connected device (desktop or mobile)
- Small amount of CELO for gas fees

**Advantages:**
- Maximum security (no single point of failure)
- Complete transparency (all transactions public)
- Customizable for network needs
- Works with any amount of funds
- Proven, widely used technology

**Limitations:**
- Requires technical onboarding for signers
- Blockchain transactions (though fast & cheap on Celo)
- Need coordination among signers
- Can be slower than centralized approach

**Support Available from ReFi Barcelona:**
- Safe setup and configuration
- Signer onboarding and training
- Creating test transactions
- Troubleshooting and office hours
- Written guides in Spanish/Catalan

**Documentation:**

### Home Community

**What It Is:**
A mobile app designed for community governance that hides all blockchain complexity. Users see euros, proposals, and thumbs up/down voting - no crypto knowledge required.

**Core Features:**
- ✅ Mobile-first (works on any smartphone)
- ✅ Simple thumbs up/down voting
- ✅ Proposals in plain language
- ✅ See amounts in euros (not crypto)
- ✅ Phone number registration (no wallet needed)
- ✅ Participatory budgeting built-in
- ✅ Community treasury dashboard
- ✅ Push notifications for proposals
- ✅ Comment on proposals

**How It Works:**
1. Download Home Community app
2. Register with phone number
3. Join your network community
4. Submit or vote on proposals
5. See results and executed decisions

**Use Cases:**
- Participatory budgeting
- Community event planning
- Resource allocation decisions
- Member engagement in governance
- Inclusive decision-making

**Network Fit:**

**La Fundición:** ★★★ **Excellent fit**
- Hides technical complexity
- Familiar interface (like social media)
- Accessible to all members
- Respects cooperative values
- No blockchain knowledge needed

**Miceli Social:** ★★ **Good for broad inclusion**
- Works if want wide participation
- Less technical control than Safe
- Could complement Safe multisig
- Good for non-technical members

**Requirements:**
- Smartphone (Android or iOS)
- Phone number
- Internet connection
- Partner setup by ReFi Barcelona

**Advantages:**
- Extremely user-friendly
- No crypto knowledge required
- Quick to onboard members
- Familiar voting interface
- Inclusive of non-technical members
- Push notifications keep people engaged

**Limitations:**

- ✅ Creates documentation for future

3. Identify key decision-making groups

**Use Cases:**

## Decision Framework

### How to Choose

**Step 1: Start with your needs** (from sense-making)
- What governance challenges did you identify?
- What would "better" look like?
- Where are the friction points?

**Step 2: Match tools to needs**

**Need:** Transparent treasury management with multiple approvers
→ **Tool:** Safe multisig

**Need:** Inclusive decision-making, easy for all members
→ **Tool:** Home Community

**Need:** Understand governance before adopting tools
→ **Tool:** IDK sessions

**Need:** Complex proposal system with many options
→ **Tool:** Gardens (explore further)

**Step 3: Consider your context**

**Technical comfort level:**
- High → Safe or Gardens
- Low → Home Community

**Network culture:**
- Tech-forward, experimental → Safe + IDK
- Territorial, cooperative values → Home Community + IDK

**Decision-making style:**
- Financial, formal → Safe
- Participatory, inclusive → Home Community
- Exploratory, mapping → IDK

**Step 4: You can combine tools**

Examples of combinations:
- **Safe for treasury + IDK for governance mapping**
- **Home Community for proposals + Safe for execution**
- **IDK first → then decide on Safe or Home Community**

### Red Flags & Green Lights

🚩 **Red Flag:** "We should use [tool] because it's cool/new"
✅ **Green Light:** "We should use [tool] because it addresses our [specific challenge]"

🚩 **Red Flag:** "Everyone else is using it"
✅ **Green Light:** "It fits our network's culture and needs"

🚩 **Red Flag:** "It does everything!"
✅ **Green Light:** "It does the specific things we need well"

🚩 **Red Flag:** "We have to adopt technology"
✅ **Green Light:** "This technology could make governance easier"

## Testing & Piloting

### Before Full Commitment

Consider piloting your selected tool with:

**Small amount:** Test with €100-500 before full Phase 2 funds

**Simple decision:** Start with low-stakes proposal (e.g., "Should we host a February meetup?")

**Limited participants:** Core group first, then expand

**Set timeline:** "We'll test for 2 weeks, then evaluate"

**Evaluation criteria:** What would success look like?

### Questions to Ask After Pilot

- ☐ Was it easier or harder than expected?
- ☐ Did it address the governance challenge we identified?
- ☐ Are members comfortable using it?
- ☐ What would we change about our approach?
- ☐ Are we ready to scale to full Phase 2 funds?
- ☐ Do we need different/additional tools?

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Do we have to choose just one tool?**
A: No! Tools can complement each other. You might use Safe for treasury management and also do IDK mapping sessions. Or Home Community for proposals and Safe for final execution.

**Q: What if we choose wrong?**
A: You can change! Phase 2 is about learning. If a tool doesn't work for your network, we can adjust. The important thing is trying something and evaluating honestly.

**Q: Can we opt out of using Web3 tools entirely?**
A: Yes. Phase 2 is about collective governance, not forcing technology. If no tools fit, we can support governance without Web3 - you make decisions, we execute, and we document the process.

**Q: How much does this cost?**
A: The tools themselves are free. Safe has tiny gas fees (cents on Celo). Home Community is free. IDK is free. ReFi Barcelona support is included in the program.

**Q: What if only some network members want to use tools?**
A: That's okay. Core governance group could use Safe, while broader network stays informed through traditional means. Or Home Community could invite all members to participate but not require it.

**Q: How long does setup take?**
A: 
- Safe: 1-2 hours for initial setup, then training
- Home Community: 30 min setup for network, then member onboarding
- IDK: Multiple 3-4 hour sessions over several weeks

**Q: What happens to Phase 2 funds if we can't decide on tools?**
A: No pressure! We can hold funds in ReFi Barcelona safe and execute based on your governance decisions made through other means while you continue exploring.
