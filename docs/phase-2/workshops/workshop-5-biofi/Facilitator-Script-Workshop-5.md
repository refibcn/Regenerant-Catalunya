## PART 2: WHAT IS BIOFI? (20 min)

### SLIDE 4: Introducing BioFi

**[VISUAL: Conceptual diagram of bioregional finance]**

**SAY:**
> "BioFi - short for Bioregional Finance or Bioregional Finance Infrastructure - is an emerging field that asks: How can we create financial systems that serve bioregional regeneration instead of extraction?
>
> It's not about creating new speculation markets. It's about channeling resources to where regenerative work is actually happening - in specific places, with specific ecosystems, by specific communities."

**CORE CONCEPTS:**

**Bio-** Bioregional
- Organized around watersheds, ecosystems, natural boundaries
- Respects local context and knowledge
- Grounds finance in place and relationships

**-Fi:** Finance Infrastructure
- Systems for channeling resources
- Transparent, accountable mechanisms
- Technology serving regenerative work

**BioFi =** Finance infrastructure for bioregional regeneration

### SLIDE 5: Why BioFi Matters

**[VISUAL: Problem/solution visual]**

**SAY:**
> "Traditional finance is extractive and disconnected from place. Capital flows to wherever returns are highest, regardless of ecological or social impact. Regenerative work - especially place-based, long-term ecosystem restoration - struggles to access capital.
>
> BioFi flips this. It asks: How can we create infrastructure where capital flows to bioregional regeneration automatically, transparently, and accountably?"

**THE CHALLENGE:**
- Regenerative work often invisible to traditional finance
- Long time horizons don't fit short-term investment models
- Place-based work hard to scale or compare
- Local knowledge disconnected from global resources

**THE OPPORTUNITY:**
- Web3 enables transparent, programmable finance
- On-chain impact reporting makes regeneration visible
- Global resources can connect to local knowledge
- New models can be tested and replicated

**BioFi creates the infrastructure** to channel resources where they're needed most.

### SLIDE 6: BioFi Principles

**[VISUAL: Principles as interconnected web or pillars]**

**SAY:**
> "BioFi is guided by several core principles that distinguish it from both traditional finance and extractive crypto:"

**SHOW:**

1. **Place-Based:** Organized around bioregions, watersheds, ecosystems
2. **Transparent:** On-chain reporting, visible impact
3. **Participatory:** Communities govern resources collectively
4. **Replicable:** Open-source templates, adaptable models
5. **Regenerative:** Capital serves ecosystem health, not extraction
6. **Cosmo-Local:** Global resources meet local knowledge

**SAY:**
> "Notice how Regenerant Catalunya embodies each of these principles. That's intentional - we designed this program as a BioFi pilot."

## Facilitator Tips & Reminders

### Tone & Approach

**This workshop is:**
- ✅ Inspirational and visionary
- ✅ Connecting local work to global movement
- ✅ Celebrating collective achievement
- ✅ Gathering honest feedback
- ✅ Looking beyond immediate program

**This workshop is NOT:**
- ❌ Sales pitch for BioFi
- ❌ Overwhelming with jargon
- ❌ Disconnected from participants' reality
- ❌ Just ReFi Barcelona talking at people
- ❌ Ignoring challenges or difficulties

### Key Messages to Emphasize

1. **You're pioneers:** Not just recipients, but co-creators
2. **Your work matters:** Local regeneration + global infrastructure
3. **Knowledge is shared:** Everything documented and open-source
4. **Infrastructure is permanent:** Tools and capacity stay with you
5. **Movement is growing:** BioFi emerging globally, you're part of it

### Managing Discussion

**If participants are quiet:**
- Use breakout rooms (pairs/small groups) first
- Share prompts in advance
- Cold call gently: "María, I'd love to hear your perspective"
- Use chat for written responses

**If discussion goes too long:**
- "This is such rich discussion. Let's continue in chat/email."
- "I'm going to note this for deeper exploration later."
- "Let's capture this feedback and move forward."

**If critical feedback emerges:**
- Thank them: "This is exactly what we need to hear."
- Don't defend: "Tell me more about that experience."
- Validate: "That sounds frustrating. Others feel this too?"
- Note for action: "We'll address this in final report/future."

### Balancing Vision & Reality

**Inspire without overselling:**
- Share possibilities honestly
- Acknowledge uncertainties
- Don't promise what can't be delivered
- Ground in concrete examples

**Connect global to local:**
- Always tie back to Catalunya context
