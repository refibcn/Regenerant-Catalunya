> We're excited to finally share publicly what we've been building: Regenerant Catalunya is a pilot program testing whether cooperative networks can use Web3 tools to create permanent infrastructure for bioregional finance.
>
> **What is Regenerant Catalunya?**
> A program supporting regenerative projects in Catalunya to experiment with Web3 governance tools, funded by Localism Fund, Gitcoin, and Celo. We're learning how to bridge cooperative values with Web3 infrastructure - not crypto for crypto's sake, but tools that serve real communities.
>
> **Phase 1 (Nov-Jan):** We supported 11 individual projects with €1k each, helping them document impact and learn Web3 basics. Projects ranged from regenerative agriculture to community organizing to circular economy initiatives.
>
> **Phase 2 (Feb-Mar):** Now we're testing network-level collective governance. Two networks - Miceli Social and La Fundició/Keras Buti - are managing shared treasuries (€6k and €5k) using Safe multisig and Hum Community. This isn't just about funding distribution. It's about building permanent infrastructure for bioregional finance.
>
> **Why This Matters:**
> Bioregional finance infrastructure (BioFi) is emerging globally - financial systems that serve regeneration instead of extraction. Regenerant Catalunya is a pilot, learning openly so other bioregions can adapt what works.

## Risk Mitigation

### Content Risks

**Risk:** Content production delayed

**Mitigation:**
- Create templates and frameworks in advance (this calendar!)
- Batch content creation when possible
- Have backup content ready (evergreen posts)
- Team can cover if one person unavailable
- Adjust calendar if needed (communication > rigid schedule)

---

**Risk:** Negative feedback or criticism

**Mitigation:**
- Honest reporting invites honest feedback (expected)
- Respond with openness and curiosity
- Use criticism to improve
- Acknowledge challenges, don't defend
- Engage constructively

---

**Risk:** Low engagement

**Mitigation:**
- Test different formats and times
- Ask followers what they want to see
- Engage actively with others' content
- Collaborate with partners for amplification
- Quality over quantity (better fewer good posts than many mediocre)

---

**Risk:** Participant concerns about representation

**Mitigation:**
- Review with participants before publishing
- Get consent for quotes and photos
- Offer anonymity option
- Adjust if concerns raised
- Prioritize accurate representation over promotional tone
