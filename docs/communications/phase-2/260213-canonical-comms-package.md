## 4) Article Insert — Section 4 (Approved Copy)

### 4. Phase Two — From Projects to Collective Governance

Phase One gave direct support to individual projects. **Phase Two shifts the remaining ~€11,000 to network-level collective governance** — giving Miceli Social and La Fundició the tools and autonomy to decide how these resources flow within their ecosystems.

This is an experiment in decentralized stewardship: whether local networks can use Web3 tools to manage funds transparently, make collective decisions, and create replicable models for other communities.

**Two pathways:**

- **Safe (multisig):** for networks comfortable with Web3 treasury operations
- **Hum Community:** for mobile-first, low-friction inclusive participation

Both pathways prioritize local control, transparency, and reusable documentation.

**Optional governance support:** Institutional Development Kit (IDK), for deeper governance mapping and protocol design.

**Current status signals:**

- Project wallets are being opened and activated
- Network leaders are engaged in planning sessions
- Tailored workshops are being scheduled

**Strategic test:** local knowledge + global infrastructure can produce better governance and allocation outcomes for bioregional finance.
