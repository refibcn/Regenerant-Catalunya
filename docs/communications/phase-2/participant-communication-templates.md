## Communication Guidelines

### Tone & Voice

**Regenerant Catalunya communications are:**
- ✅ Honest (not promotional)
- ✅ Accessible (not jargony)
- ✅ Respectful (honoring participants)
- ✅ Educational (sharing methodology)
- ✅ Warm (personal, not corporate)
- ✅ Bilingual (Spanish/Catalan consideration)

**Avoid:**
- ❌ Over-hype or exaggeration
- ❌ Blockchain jargon without explanation
- ❌ Defensive tone when discussing challenges
- ❌ Talking at participants (talk with them)
- ❌ Promotional language ("amazing! incredible!")

---

### Language Considerations

**Spanish Default:**
- Most participants fluent in Spanish
- Comfortable for written communication
- Some participants native Spanish speakers

**Catalan Availability:**
- Important for local context and respect
- Provide key documents in Catalan
- Note Catalan version available
- Use Catalan greetings/closings

**Example:**
- Email in Spanish
- Note: "Versión en català disponible aquí: [link]"
- Or: Close with "Gràcies / Gracias"

**Code-Switching:**
- Acceptable to use both languages naturally
- Reflects Catalunya multilingual reality
- Participants comfortable with this

---

### Formatting Best Practices

**Email Structure:**
- Clear subject line (what + when if applicable)
- Personal greeting ("Hola [Name]" not "Dear participant")
- Brief opening paragraph (context + purpose)
- Scannable sections with headers
- Bullet points and lists
- Clear next steps or CTAs
- Warm, personal closing
- Complete contact info

**Visual Hierarchy:**
- **Headers:** Clear section breaks
- **Bold:** Key information, dates, actions
- *Italics:* Emphasis, nuance
- Bullets: Lists, options, choices
- Numbers: Steps, sequences, rankings

**Links:**
- Descriptive text, not raw URLs
- "Download guide here" not "https://..."
- Test all links before sending
- Use short URLs for printed materials

**Attachments:**
- Keep small (<2MB total)
- PDFs for documents
- Name clearly: "Workshop-4-Agenda-Spanish.pdf"
- Mention in email body

---

### Timing & Cadence

**Regular Communications:**
- Monthly update: First week of following month
- Workshop invitations: 1 week before
- Workshop reminders: 24 hours before
- Post-workshop follow-up: Within 48 hours

**Ad Hoc Communications:**
- Important announcements: As needed
- Urgent support: Immediate
- Deadline reminders: 3 days and 1 day before
- Results/celebrations: As they happen

**Avoid:**
- Too frequent (overwhelm)
- Too rare (disconnect)
- Irregular without pattern (confusing)
- Last-minute always (shows poor planning)

**Balance:**
- 2-4 emails per month to participants (appropriate)
- Daily social media (different audience)
- Weekly newsletter or less (deep-dive content)
