# Project Spotlight: Fluvià River Basin Restoration

_This is the first in our series spotlighting the 11 regenerative projects supported by Regenerant Catalunya._

## The Project

The **Fluvià River Basin Restoration** project is working to conserve and restore natural ecosystems in one of Catalonia's vital waterways. The Fluvià River flows through northeastern Catalonia, supporting diverse ecosystems and local communities.

**Their regenerative work includes:**

### Ecosystem Restoration

- Restoring native vegetation along riverbanks
- Improving water quality through natural filtration systems
- Supporting biodiversity by creating habitat for native species
- Addressing erosion and sedimentation issues

### Community Engagement

- Involving local communities in conservation efforts
- Building awareness of river ecosystems and their importance
- Creating educational programs about water stewardship
- Facilitating community-led restoration activities

### Impact Measurement

- Documenting restoration activities and their outcomes
- Tracking environmental indicators (water quality, biodiversity, vegetation cover)
- Measuring community participation and engagement
- Creating baseline data for long-term impact assessment

## The Challenge

Projects like Fluvià River Basin Restoration are doing essential regenerative work, but they face common challenges:

**Funding Access**

- Traditional grant applications are time-consuming and competitive
- Many funders don't understand the long-term nature of ecosystem restoration
- Limited funding for "maintenance" activities vs. new initiatives

**Impact Documentation**

- Difficult to demonstrate impact when restoration takes years
- Lack of standardized metrics for ecosystem restoration
- Limited resources for comprehensive impact measurement

**Network Isolation**

- Working in isolation without connection to other regenerative projects
- Missing opportunities for knowledge sharing and collaboration
- Limited visibility to potential funders and supporters

## How Regenerant Catalunya Supports Their Work

### Phase 1 Funding

Projects receive **€1,000-1,500** in Phase 1 to support ongoing restoration activities. This flexible funding allows projects to:

- Continue critical restoration work
- Invest in impact measurement tools
- Build capacity for future funding

### Capacity Building

Projects are learning to document their impact transparently using **KarmaGap**, creating a verifiable, on-chain record of their regenerative activities. This "living resume" will help them:

- Demonstrate impact to future funders
- Build credibility in the regenerative finance space
- Access new funding opportunities

### Web3 Tools

By setting up Web3 wallets and learning to use regenerative finance tools, projects are building infrastructure for:

- Receiving funding from anywhere in the world
- Transparent financial management
- Future participation in Web3 funding rounds
- Integration with impact verification tools (like Silvi for tree planting)

### Network Connection

Through the program, projects connect with other regenerative initiatives across Catalonia, creating opportunities for:

- Knowledge sharing and collaboration
- Collective advocacy
- Network-level governance (Phase 2)
- Ongoing support and community

## The Impact

With Regenerant Catalunya's support, Fluvià River Basin Restoration can:

- Continue and expand restoration activities
- Build a verifiable record of their impact
- Access new funding opportunities
- Connect with a network of regenerative projects
- Create sustainable funding infrastructure

## What's Next

In Phase 2, projects like Fluvià River Basin Restoration will participate in network-level collective governance, helping decide how additional funds are allocated across the Miceli Social network. This creates a model for ongoing bioregional regeneration.
