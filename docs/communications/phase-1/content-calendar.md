## Publishing Guidelines

### LinkedIn

- **Best time:** Tuesday-Thursday, 8-10 AM or 12-1 PM CET
- **Length:** 1,300-2,000 characters optimal
- **Hashtags:** 3-5 relevant hashtags
- **Format:** Professional, detailed explanations

### Twitter/X

- **Best time:** Tuesday-Thursday, 9-11 AM or 2-4 PM CET
- **Format:** Threads for educational content (5-10 tweets)
- **Hashtags:** 2-3 relevant hashtags
- **Engagement:** Reply to comments, engage with community

### Blue Sky

- **Best time:** Tuesday-Thursday, 10 AM - 2 PM CET
- **Format:** Visual-first, brief updates
- **Length:** Shorter than Twitter, more visual
- **Engagement:** Community-focused, casual tone

### Newsletter

- **Publish day:** Friday morning CET
- **Format:** Long-form articles, comprehensive coverage
- **Length:** 800-1,500 words
- **Structure:** Clear sections, links to resources
