# Introducing Regenerant Catalunya: Building Infrastructure for Bioregional Regeneration

_We're excited to announce the launch of Regenerant Catalunya, a participatory funding round connecting regenerative projects across Catalonia with global Web3 infrastructure._

## What is Regenerant Catalunya?

**Regenerant Catalunya** is a two-phase funding program that supports regenerative work—from ecosystem restoration to urban community infrastructure, food sovereignty to housing cooperatives—while building capacity for ongoing bioregional regeneration.

Rather than simply providing funding, this program combines direct project support with comprehensive capacity building, creating infrastructure that projects will use for years to come.

## The Funding Model

### €29,200 Total Funding Pool

Through collaboration between local partners and global Web3 funders, we've created a replicable funding model:

- **€11,000** from local partners (Miceli Social, La Fundició/Keras Buti)
- **€18,200** matched by global Web3 funders via Localism Fund
- **Total:** €29,200 supporting 11 projects

This model demonstrates that local commitment can be amplified by global support, creating sustainable funding infrastructure for bioregional regeneration.

### Two-Phase Structure

**Phase 1 (Nov-Dec 2025): Baseline Allocation**

- Each project receives **€1,000-1,500** based on minimum participation requirements
- Projects learn to use **KarmaGap** for on-chain activity and impact reporting
- Capacity-building workshops cover Web3 fundamentals, impact measurement, and collaborative governance

**Phase 2 (Jan-Feb 2026): Network-Level Governance**

- Additional **€1,000 per project** allocated to network-level pools
- **~€6,000** for Miceli Social network (rural projects)
- **~€5,000** for La Fundició/Keras Buti network (urban projects)
- Networks collectively govern these funds using Web3 governance tools

## The Approach

### Capacity Building + Funding

This program isn't just about providing funding—it's about building capacity for ongoing regenerative finance. Projects learn:

- **Web3 Wallet Setup** — Secure fund management with social recovery
- **Impact Documentation** — Transparent, on-chain activity reporting using KarmaGap
- **Regenerative Measurement** — Setting metrics and tracking outcomes
- **Collective Governance** — Network-level decision-making using Web3 tools

### Building Within Trusted Ecosystems

Rather than imposing external solutions, we work within established networks:

- **Miceli Social** — Rural regenerative projects across Catalonia
- **La Fundició/Keras Buti** — Urban regenerative projects in Barcelona area

This approach leverages existing relationships and social infrastructure, enabling rapid project engagement and sustainable integration.

### Transparent Impact Documentation

All projects document their regenerative activities on-chain using **KarmaGap**, creating:

- Verifiable records of regenerative work
- Credibility for future funding applications
- Evidence for policy advocacy
- Learning resources for other projects

### Open-Source Knowledge

The program creates replicable models and open-source knowledge products that can be adapted globally, proving blockchain technology can serve real community needs.

## The Projects

We're supporting **11 regenerative projects** across Catalonia, representing diverse regenerative work:

**Rural Regenerative Work:**

- Ecosystem restoration (Fluvià River Basin)
- Sustainable agriculture
- Community-led conservation

**Urban Regenerative Work:**

- Food sovereignty (La Olla Community Kitchen)
- Housing cooperatives (Las Yuntas)
- Textile sovereignty (Laurel 31)
- Community infrastructure

Each project represents essential regenerative work that builds community resilience and addresses systemic challenges.

## Why This Matters

### Building Infrastructure, Not Just Providing Funding

Projects aren't just receiving funding—they're learning tools and methodologies they'll use for years to come:

- KarmaGap profiles for future funding applications
- Web3 wallets for participation in global funding rounds
- Impact documentation that builds credibility
- Network connections for ongoing collaboration

### Creating Replicable Models

The Regenerant Catalunya approach can be adapted by other bioregions globally:

- Open-source knowledge products
- Documented methodologies
- Replicable funding structure
- Transferable capacity-building approach

### Bridging Local and Global

We've demonstrated that traditional cooperative values can integrate seamlessly with cutting-edge Web3 tools:

- Local knowledge + global infrastructure
- Trusted ecosystems + innovative tools
- Community values + transparent technology
- Bioregional regeneration + global networks

# Workshop 1 Preview: Introducing Web3 Tools to Regenerative Projects

_Following up on our program announcement, we're excited to introduce 11 regenerative projects across Catalonia to Web3 tools that will enable transparent fund management and impact documentation._

As we prepare for our first capacity-building workshop, we're introducing projects to Web3 wallets—the foundation for transparent fund management and impact documentation in Regenerant Catalunya.

## Why Web3 Wallets Matter for Regenerative Finance

### Social Recovery: Reducing Risk for Organizations

Unlike traditional cryptocurrency wallets, modern Web3 wallets like **Valora** and **Minipay** include social recovery features. This means if a project loses access to their wallet (forgets password, loses device), they can recover it through trusted contacts.

**Why this matters:** For organizations new to Web3, the risk of losing funds due to lost keys has been a major barrier. Social recovery makes Web3 tools accessible to regenerative projects that can't afford to lose funding due to technical issues.

### Transparent Fund Management: Building Trust

All transactions on Web3 wallets are visible on-chain, creating unprecedented accountability and trust. Funders can see exactly how their contributions are being used, and projects can demonstrate transparent financial management.

**Why this matters:** Traditional grant reporting often happens months after funds are spent. With on-chain transactions, impact is visible in real-time, building trust with funders and communities.

### Global Access: Opening New Funding Streams

Web3 wallets enable projects to receive funding from anywhere in the world without traditional banking barriers. This opens new funding opportunities for regenerative work that might not fit traditional grant categories.

**Why this matters:** Many regenerative projects struggle to access international funding due to banking restrictions, currency conversion issues, or lack of traditional organizational structures. Web3 wallets remove these barriers.

### Tool Integration: Building Verifiable Impact Records

Wallets connect to platforms like **KarmaGap** for impact reporting, enabling projects to build a verifiable, on-chain record of their regenerative activities. This creates a "living resume" that demonstrates impact to future funders.

**Why this matters:** Impact documentation is often time-consuming and disconnected from actual work. Web3 tools integrate documentation into the workflow, making it easier for projects to demonstrate their regenerative impact.

## What Projects Will Learn in Workshop 1

**Setting up a Web3 wallet on Celo**

- Choosing between Valora, Minipay, and other options
- Understanding the differences and use cases
- Step-by-step setup guidance

**Understanding wallet security and social recovery**

- How to set up social recovery contacts
- Best practices for key management
- Organizational wallet management strategies

**Receiving and managing funds transparently**

- How to receive funds from the multisig wallet
- Understanding on-chain transactions
- Converting to fiat currency when needed

**Connecting wallets to impact reporting tools**

- WalletConnect integration with KarmaGap
- Building verifiable impact records
- Creating transparent project documentation

**Best practices for organizational fund management**

- Multi-signature considerations
- Team access and security
- Integration with existing financial systems

## The Approach: Building Capacity, Not Just Teaching Technology

We're not just teaching technology—we're building capacity for ongoing regenerative finance. Projects will use these tools beyond this program, creating infrastructure for:

- Future funding rounds
- Transparent impact documentation
- Network-level collective governance (Phase 2)
- Ongoing bioregional regeneration

## Workshop Format

**Duration:** 1.5 hours

- 60 minutes: Presentation with strategic Q&A breaks
- 30 minutes: Breakout groups for wallet setup support
- Split into 3 groups of 3-4 projects each for personalized assistance

**Language:** Spanish (with materials available in Catalan)

**Support:** Follow-up office hours available for projects needing additional help

## What's Next

After Workshop 1, projects will have their wallets set up and be ready to receive Phase 1 funding. Workshop 2 will dive deep into KarmaGap for impact documentation, and Workshop 3 will focus on impact metrics and regenerative measurement.

This is just the beginning of building Web3 capacity for regenerative work in Catalonia.

# Workshop 2 Recap: Building Verifiable Impact Documentation with KarmaGap

_Workshop 2 of Regenerant Catalunya's Phase 1 capacity-building program focused on impact documentation using KarmaGap—an on-chain activity and impact reporting platform._

## What is KarmaGap?

**KarmaGap** (recently rebranded as "Karma") is an on-chain activity and impact reporting platform that enables projects to create a living, public "project resume"—making their regenerative impact visible and verifiable to funders, communities, and the broader regenerative finance ecosystem.

Unlike traditional impact reporting that happens in isolated documents or spreadsheets, KarmaGap creates permanent, verifiable records on-chain that can be accessed by anyone, anytime.

## Why Impact Documentation Matters for Regenerative Finance

### Transparent Impact Reporting

All activities and impact metrics are recorded on-chain, creating verifiable records that funders can trust. This transparency:

- Builds credibility with funders
- Enables real-time impact tracking
- Creates accountability without bureaucracy
- Demonstrates long-term commitment to regenerative work

### Tool Integration

KarmaGap connects with other regenerative finance tools, creating an integrated ecosystem:

**COP (Community Operating Protocol)** — For community governance data and decision-making records

**Eco Certs/Hypercerts** — For impact credentials and verification, enabling projects to "mint" their impact achievements

**Silvi** — For tree planting and forest restoration MRV (Measurement, Reporting, Verification), enabling projects to verify and monetize carbon sequestration

This integration means projects don't have to use multiple disconnected systems—everything connects in a verifiable, on-chain ecosystem.

### Global Visibility

Projects can showcase their impact to funders worldwide, opening new funding opportunities beyond traditional grant cycles. This is especially valuable for:

- International funders looking for verified impact
- Web3-native funding rounds
- Impact investors seeking transparent metrics
- Communities wanting to verify project claims

### Long-term Value

Impact documentation becomes a permanent record that projects can use for years to come. This creates:

- A verifiable history of regenerative work
- Credibility for future funding applications
- Evidence for policy advocacy
- Learning resources for other projects

## What Projects Learned in Workshop 2

### Creating Project Profiles

Projects set up their profiles on KarmaGap, including:

- Project description and mission
- Location and bioregional context
- Team and organizational structure
- Contact information and links

### Documenting Activities

Projects learned to document their regenerative activities with:

- **Dates** — Start and end dates for activities
- **Outputs** — What was produced or accomplished
- **Deliverables** — Specific results or products
