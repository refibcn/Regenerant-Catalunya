## Overview

The **Localism Fund Expert Network** is a curated, peer‑attested collective of practitioners advancing the intersection of **localism** and **Ethereum‑based coordination**. Experts in the network bring proven experience across one or more of the following domains:

1. **Grant‑making** — designing, managing, or evaluating grant programs in Web3, philanthropy, civic, or public sectors.
2. **Web3 / Ethereum Tooling** — proficiency with tools for coordination, funding, governance, or identity (e.g., Karma GAP, Gardens, Giveth, Allo, Prosperity Pass).
3. **Localism** — hands‑on experience building or advising initiatives advancing political, economic, cultural, ecological, or Ethereum localism.

Collectively, this network functions as the connective tissue between global infrastructure and local action — ensuring that every Localism Fund initiative benefits from both high‑level expertise and grounded community context.

## Local Grant Programs | Round 01

The **Local Grant Programs | Round 01** marks the first active use case of the Expert Network. This round provides the initial proving ground for how expert evaluation, mentorship, and feedback loops can strengthen the quality, integrity, and impact of locally led Ethereum funding programs. The network’s role extends beyond traditional application review: experts will help assess, advise, and amplify local hubs as they design and deliver credible local funding programs rooted in political, economic, cultural, and ecological localism.

### Expert Roles (Round 01)

#### 1) Application Evaluation & Intelligence

Evaluate and score applications using the official Localism Fund Evaluation Rubric while also providing structured qualitative feedback to guide applicants. Experts help surface collective intelligence — identifying credible, high‑potential programs and advising where additional support is needed.

**Application Submission (Karma GAP Platform)**  
Prospective grantees submit applications through Karma GAP, which serves as the primary application management system:

- **Structured Application Process:** comprehensive forms aligned with evaluation rubric categories.
- **Progress Tracking:** real‑time visibility into application status and evaluation progress.

**Evaluation Rubric — Scoring**  
Experts independently score applications across four categories:

- **Program Scope & Strategic Alignment (30%)**
- **Local Co‑Funding & Financial Sustainability (30%)**
- **Impact Measurement & Web3 Integration (20%)**
- **Team Capacity & Community Anchoring (20%)**

Each criterion is scored **1–4**, with qualitative comments required for transparency and learning. **Half‑points** may be used for nuance.

**Evaluation Matching Framework**

- Up to **2× matching awarded**, max **$20k** per grantee.
- **Total Score: 13–16** → ~**2× matching** — strong candidate for approval and higher allocation.
- **Total Score: 10–12** → ~**1.5× matching** — qualified candidate with standard allocation consideration.
- **Total Score: 7–9** → conditional approval pending improvements.
- **Total Score: below 7** → requires significant development before reapplication.

_Exception:_ any additional round funds not disbursed will be allocated to future rounds.

**Evaluation Flow**

1. **Pairing of Applications:** each application is paired to **3–4 experts**, balancing geography, language, and expertise alignment.
2. **Scoring & Feedback:** experts submit rubric scores and written insights through a **Notion database** (to be provided).
3. **Threshold & Pairing:** applications reaching a **minimum viable readiness score** _(e.g., ≥ 7 points average)_ become eligible for mentorship pairing and potential funding.

#### 2) Mentorship & Advisory

Beyond evaluations, the network provides hands‑on guidance and support to local hubs, particularly those demonstrating readiness but needing targeted assistance. Each selected local program may be paired with **1–3 experts** aligned to its needs (e.g., governance design, technical setup, grant operations, impact reporting).

Focus areas:

1. Reviewing and refining governance and funding mechanisms.
2. Sharing guidance, playbooks, and best practices for community‑led grant operations.
3. Providing hands‑on help with Web3 tools (e.g., Karma GAP setup, Gardens configuration, Prosperity Pass integration).
4. Offering operational mentorship to enhance delivery capacity, accountability, and community trust.
5. Supporting alignment between the hub’s local priorities and Ethereum’s broader coordination ecosystem.

Mentorship is **voluntary** and **self‑directed** — experts decide the depth that is most meaningful and sustainable, from light‑touch advisory to deeper collaboration.

**Mentorship Pairing** emerges organically as experts engage with applications. Operators coordinate and record pairings to ensure balance and clear communication.

**Pairing Criteria:** topical fit; tool alignment; geographic/cultural context; availability & interest.

#### 3) Tooling Feedback & System Evolution

Experts help bridge local implementers and Web3 tool builders by providing feedback on:

1. Tool **usability**, documentation, onboarding flows.
2. **Technical/operational** bottlenecks faced by hubs.
3. Opportunities for **interoperability/integration** across tools used by different hubs.

This feeds a consolidated **Tooling & System Learning Report** to inform improvements for future rounds and ecosystem infrastructure.

## Contribution Tiers & Compensation

Expert contributions to Round 01 are intentionally flexible — from deep mentorship with a few local programs to light‑touch evaluation across many, or ecosystem‑level feedback that improves round design and tooling.

### Compensation Overview

- **Total Pool:** **$5,500 USD + 10,000 CELO**, distributed via **Prosperity Pass**.
- **Distribution:** finalized **January 2026** as **retroactive + prospective** funding.
- **Localism Fund Expert Badge (on‑chain):** each expert receives an EAS‑based credential reflecting their contribution tier. When used on **Prosperity Pass**, it boosts reputation, unlocks eligibility for future rewards, and creates a portable record recognized across rounds and networks.

### Contribution Tiers _(guidance for fair self‑assessment)_

| Tier                                  | Typical Contribution Profile                                                                                                                                                              | Estimated Hours | Compensation Range                      |
| ------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------: | --------------------------------------- |
| **Tier 1 – Light Contribution**       | 3–5 reviews with meaningful feedback; participate in discussions or async calls; one‑off hub support                                                                                      |        ~**3–6** | **$200–$400** + Expert Badge (Tier 1)   |
| **Tier 2 – Significant Contribution** | 6–8 full reviews with written feedback; ongoing mentorship to 1–2 programs; calibration sessions; structured tooling feedback                                                             |       ~**8–12** | **$400–$700** + Expert Badge (Tier 2)   |
| **Tier 3 – Heavy Contribution**       | 8–10+ in‑depth reviews **or** deep mentorship (ops co‑design, tooling setup, impact measurement); lead tooling reports or round governance contributions; document & synthesize learnings |     ~**12–20+** | **$700–$1,200** + Expert Badge (Tier 3) |

### Expert Selection & Compensation Policy

**Tier Pre‑Selection** — After reviewing applications, **Network Initiators** suggest a provisional tier (1/2/3) based on experience, availability, and proposed engagement—ensuring budget alignment and clear expectations.

**Dynamic Adjustment** — Provisional tier ≠ guaranteed payout. At the end of the round:

- Under‑delivery → lower‑end payout or move down a tier.
- Exceeding expectations → higher‑end payout or move up a tier (subject to budget).

**Evaluation of Contribution** — Experts self‑assess using: scope of work; time & effort; quality & impact; fairness to peers; integrity; optional personal circumstances. Final confirmation is **consent‑based** with Operators.

**Final Payouts Consider**: number/quality of reviews; depth/helpfulness of feedback; mentorship & community support; tooling/documentation/system design contributions; reliability & collaboration.

**Transparency & Fairness** — Provisional tiers known at start; final adjustments reviewed **Jan 2026** to ensure consistency and accountability.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What is the Localism Fund Expert Network?**  
A peer‑to‑peer attested network of trusted experts supporting credible local networks and place‑based groups in political, economic, cultural, and ecological localism — demonstrating Ethereum as foundational infrastructure.

**Who can join the network?**  
Anyone with demonstrated expertise in grant‑making, Web3/Ethereum tooling, or localism. Applicants must uphold OpenCivics Ethical Standards and complete peer validation via TrustGraph.

**How do I become a validated expert?**

1. Fill out the form → _Localism Fund | Become a Peer Validated Expert_; 2) Receive peer attestations via TrustGraph; 3) Once your TrustScore crosses the threshold, you’re recognized as a **Validated Expert**, eligible to contribute to active rounds.

**What is TrustGraph?**  
An on‑chain attestation network mapping relationships of trust and expertise. Members can attest to peers; a PageRank‑style algorithm computes dynamic **TrustScores** for validation.

**What’s the longer‑term vision?**  
A standing guild of evaluators and mentors serving multiple Localism Fund rounds and partners — growing into an open, interlinked **expert commons** for localism‑oriented funding ecosystems (bioregional governance, ecological finance, civic tech, public digital infrastructure, onchain coordination protocols, etc.).

**Is there compensation for experts?**  
Yes. For Round 01: total pool **$5,500 USD + 10,000 CELO**. Compensation follows contribution tiers (**$200–$1,200 + badges**).

**How much time is expected?**  
Flexible: from no engagement (lurking) to light (~3–6 hours) or deep (~12–20+ hours) involvement per active cycle.

**What is Prosperity Pass?**  
A smart‑account wallet and reputation layer (Ethereum & Celo). The **Localism Fund Expert Badge** can be claimed on Prosperity Pass as: proof of expertise; portable credential; gateway to future rewards, governance participation, and reputation growth.

**Where can I ask questions or get support?**  
Join the community on **Telegram** → <https://t.me/localismfund> or email **support@localism.fund**.
