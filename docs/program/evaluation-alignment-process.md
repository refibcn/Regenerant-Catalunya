# Evaluation Criteria Alignment Process

**Purpose:** Ensure projects understand evaluation criteria and can align their documentation accordingly.

---

## Process Overview

**Timeline:**
- **Pre-Workshop 2:** Share criteria, schedule alignment calls
- **During Workshop 2:** Dedicated module on criteria
- **Post-Workshop 2:** Self-assessment, feedback, final alignment

---

## Post-Workshop 2 Alignment

### Step 1: Self-Assessment (Dec 8-14)

**Action:** Projects complete self-assessment in data collection document

**Location:** Section F: Evaluation Alignment in project data collection document

**What to Assess:**
- Score (self-assessment) for each criterion (1-10)
- Strengths identified
- Evidence available
- Gaps identified
- Questions for evaluators

**Support:**
- Template provided in data collection document
- Guidance available during office hours
- Individual check-ins if needed

### Step 2: Review Self-Assessments (Dec 15-19)

**Action:** ReFi Barcelona team reviews self-assessments

**Process:**
1. Review each project's self-assessment
2. Identify common patterns or concerns
3. Note projects that may need extra support
4. Prepare feedback and guidance

**Focus Areas:**
- Projects scoring themselves very low (may need encouragement)
- Projects with significant gaps (may need guidance)
- Projects with questions (need answers)
- Projects ready to proceed (confirm they're on track)

### Step 3: Provide Feedback (Dec 15-19)

**Format:**
- Individual feedback via comments in data collection document
- Group feedback during office hours
- Individual check-ins if needed

**Feedback Should:**
- Acknowledge strengths
- Address gaps constructively
- Answer questions
- Provide guidance on documentation
- Encourage and support

**Tone:**
- Supportive and constructive
- Focus on helping projects succeed
- Clarify, don't criticize

### Step 4: Final Alignment (Dec 20-24)

**Action:** Ensure projects understand what's expected

**Process:**
1. Follow up with projects that had questions
2. Confirm understanding
3. Provide final guidance before Karma submission
4. Address any remaining concerns

**Check-in Questions:**
- Do you understand what evaluators are looking for?
- Do you know how to align your documentation?
- Are you ready to submit to Karma?
- Do you have any remaining questions?

## Common Questions & Answers

### Q: What if my project doesn't fit all criteria?

**A:** That's okay! Focus on the criteria that align with your work. All projects have strengths in different areas. Document what you do well.

### Q: How detailed does my documentation need to be?

**A:** Quality over quantity. Clear, specific documentation with evidence is better than vague, lengthy descriptions. Focus on measurable impact where possible.

### Q: What if I don't have numbers/metrics?

**A:** That's fine! Qualitative impact is also valuable. Describe outcomes, changes, and benefits clearly. Photos and stories can be powerful evidence.

### Q: How much does Web3 adoption matter?

**A:** It's 25% of the evaluation. Meeting minimum requirements (wallet, Karma) is important, but you don't need to be a Web3 expert. Focus on using the tools provided.

### Q: What if my project is small/new?

**A:** That's considered! Criterion 3 (Resource Efficiency) rewards strong performance relative to project size. Small projects with big impact score well.
