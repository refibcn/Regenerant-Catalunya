# Tech Stack Tool Summaries

## 1. Project-Level Tools

### Required (Phase 1)

- **Karma GAP** – On-chain activity and impact reporting platform creating a public "project resume" that makes impact visible and verifiable.

- **Web3 wallet on Celo** – Secure self-custodial wallets for fund management. Preferably with social recovery (Valora, Minipay). Also supports MetaMask, Zerion, Rainbow.

### Optional Experiments

- **Silvi** – Tree planting dMRV platform for transparent tracking of reforestation projects with on-chain verification.

- **Gainforest** – Decentralized fund using AI to measure and reward sustainable nature stewardship. Enables trustless donations to Indigenous communities and forest conservation projects with automated fund releases upon AI-verified milestones and dynamic impact certificates (NFTrees).

- **Hypercerts/Ecocerts** – Impact credential systems creating verifiable, tradable certificates representing positive impacts. Hypercerts for general impact, Ecocerts for environmental and sustainability certifications.

## 2. Network-Level Tools (Phase 2)

- **Safe (multisig)** – Multi-signature wallet requiring multiple approvals for secure, transparent treasury management.

- **Gardens** – Conviction voting platform for community funding and governance using time-weighted voting.

- **Sarafu** – Local currency and commitment pooling platform for community digital currencies and collective projects.

- **Cycles** – Open clearing protocol for mutual credit and circular economy, enabling value exchange without external currency.
