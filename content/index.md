<div class="section-container">
  <div class="section-content-centered">
    <h2 class="section-title">How It Works</h2>
    <p class="section-subtitle">
      Regenerant Catalunya uses a two-phase funding mechanism that combines direct project support with network-level collective governance, building capacity and infrastructure for ongoing bioregional regeneration.
    </p>
  </div>
  
  <div class="section-content-wide">
    <dl class="two-col-grid" style="margin-top: 2rem;">
      <div class="grid-item" style="background: var(--green-highlight); padding: 1.5rem;">
        <dt style="font-size: 1.125rem; margin-bottom: 0.75rem; margin-top: 0;"><strong>Phase 1: Baseline Allocation
        <br><em>Nov-Dec 2025</em></strong></dt>
        <dd style="margin: 0; font-size: 0.95rem; line-height: 1.6;">
          Each project receives <strong>€1,000-€1,500</strong> based on minimum participation requirements and simplified impact evaluation.
          <br>
          Projects use <strong>Karma GAP</strong> for on-chain activity and impact reporting.
        </dd>
      </div>
      <div class="grid-item" style="background: var(--green-highlight); padding: 1.5rem;">
        <dt style="font-size: 1.125rem; margin-bottom: 0.75rem; margin-top: 0;"><strong>Phase 2: Network-Level Governance
        <br><em>Jan-Feb 2026</em></strong></dt>
        <dd style="margin: 0; font-size: 0.95rem; line-height: 1.6;">
          An additional <strong>€1,000 per project</strong> is allocated to network-level pools: <strong>~€6,000 for Miceli Social network</strong> and <strong>~€5,000 for La Fundició/Keras Buti network</strong>.
          <br>
          Networks collectively govern these funds using Web3 tools like <strong>Gardens</strong>, <strong>Safe</strong>, <strong>Sarafu Network</strong>, and <strong>Cycles</strong> <em>(see more below).</em>
        </dd>
      </div>
    </dl>
    <div class="three-col-grid" style="margin-top: 2rem;">
      <div class="grid-item">
        <h4 style="margin-top: 0; color: var(--darkgray); font-size: 1.125rem;">Capacity Building & Support</h4>
        <p style="color: var(--darkgray); font-size: 0.95rem; line-height: 1.6; margin-bottom: 0;">
          Projects receive comprehensive capacity-building workshops covering Web3 fundamentals, impact measurement, and collaborative governance, plus technical mentorship from global partners.
        </p>
      </div>
      <div class="grid-item">
        <h4 style="margin-top: 0; color: var(--darkgray); font-size: 1.125rem;">Building Within Trusted Ecosystems</h4>
        <p style="color: var(--darkgray); font-size: 0.95rem; line-height: 1.6; margin-bottom: 0;">
          Rather than imposing external solutions, we work within established networks—Miceli Social (rural) and La Fundició/Keras Buti (urban)—leveraging existing relationships and social infrastructure.
        </p>
      </div>
      <div class="grid-item">
        <h4 style="margin-top: 0; color: var(--darkgray); font-size: 1.125rem;">Open-Source Knowledge</h4>
        <p style="color: var(--darkgray); font-size: 0.95rem; line-height: 1.6; margin-bottom: 0;">
          The program creates replicable models and open-source knowledge products that can be adapted globally, proving blockchain technology can serve real community needs.
        </p>
      </div>
    </div>
    <div style="margin-top: 2rem; padding: 1.5rem 2rem; background: var(--green-highlight); border: 2px solid var(--green-dark);">
      <h3 style="margin-top: 0; margin-bottom: 0.75rem; color: var(--darkgray); font-size: 1.25rem;">At Least 80% Flows Directly to Projects</h3>
      <p style="margin-bottom: 0; color: var(--darkgray); line-height: 1.6;">
        The program design ensures that at least 80% of the total budget flows directly to projects and their networks, split 50/50 between the two phases. This creates sustainable pathways for communities to integrate new tools while building ongoing infrastructure for bioregional regeneration.
      </p>
    </div>
